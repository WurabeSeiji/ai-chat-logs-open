# Re-weaving spacetime from frequency alone — a thought experiment on a model particle (Papers 12 and 13)

## What this article is about

If we take, as fundamental, neither time nor space but a single quantity — the frequency ν — how can the familiar structures of physics be laid out anew? Two observational papers (thought experiments) pursue this on a single abstract model particle.

- Paper 12: why the oscillatory degrees of freedom are four (the floor of phase-space area, and the dimensions of division algebras).
- Paper 13: what happens when energy accompanies the four (the fifth frequency and the composite curvature).

Neither paper modifies standard quantum theory, general relativity, or special relativity, and neither proposes any new prediction or theorem. They are records that merely lay out known mathematics and observe it from the single viewpoint of frequency.

## Paper 12: why the oscillatory degrees of freedom are four

The starting point is two pieces of established mathematics of entirely different origin.

- The Robertson uncertainty floor: phase space has no zero-area point-like state; a minimal symplectic area √det Σ ≥ 1/2 remains as a floor (a continuous constraint; de Gosson's quantum blob).
- The Hurwitz–Frobenius series: the only division algebras whose norm is multiplicative are the reals, complexes, quaternions, and octonions, of dimensions 1, 2, 4, 8 (a discrete constraint).

Laying these two side by side under the single viewpoint of phase-space area, the continuous floor (cannot become a point) and the discrete ring (composition of axes closes) both appear to point at dimension 4.

- Because of the floor, a state cannot collapse to a point and must possess an intrinsic oscillatory degree of freedom.
- The minimal dimension in which the composition of those oscillation axes closes (satisfying divisibility, non-commutativity, associativity) is, among 1, 2, 4, 8, the quaternions ℍ (dimension four).

From these two, the number of intrinsic oscillatory degrees of freedom is admitted to be four — this structural coincidence is what Paper 12 records.

Paper 12 there obtained a formal four-component quantity lining up the four dependent quantities, but, holding that its becoming a physical four-momentum requires a separate introduction of the Minkowski metric, deliberately set aside the sign structure.

## Paper 13: what happens when energy accompanies the four

Paper 13 restarts one step before that set-aside part — from where and how energy appears among the four components.

### 1. The zero-sum condition formally calls forth a fifth direction

In a system with neither background spacetime nor an absolute scale, when positive energy accompanies the four, we impose, as a thought experiment, the conservation-type condition that the whole quadratic form vanishes. This is not a Noether conservation law derived from time-translation symmetry, but a condition imposed formally on this system (not a derivation from standard theory).

Since there is no canceling partner among the four, a fifth direction ν₅ carrying the negative part appears formally, and one obtains

ν₁² + ν₂² + ν₃² + ν₄² − ν₅² = 0.

This single expression has three faces.

- The imposed zero-sum conservation condition itself.
- A five-dimensional light-cone null condition of signature (+,+,+,+,−).
- Seen from four dimensions, the same quadratic form as the mass shell E² − p² = m².

But ν₅ is not identified with mass. Taking the term-by-term correspondence precisely, the isolated axis ν₅ (of opposite sign) sits at the position of the energy term E² of the standard mass shell, and the mass-term position is occupied by one of the four. Conversely, if one places ν₅ at the mass term, under the signature count (4,1) one gets, not a shell, but a sphere S³, and the correspondence breaks. This agrees with the Kaluza–Klein-type reading (an extra direction appears as mass in lower dimension).

The phrase "imaginary direction" is likewise only a convenient name for the minus sign of the metric signature, not a complexification of ν₅.

### 2. The central projection taking the fifth direction as radius, and its cut

We formulate, purely geometrically and algebraically, the central projection taking ν₅ as radius. Setting each axis to its reciprocal x_i = 1/ν_i (a notational definition, not a claim of a physical image) and R = 1/ν₅, we project centrally onto a sphere of radius R.

The operation on a subsequent axis is not a central projection but a cut on the sphere (the central projection is performed once only). The composite curvature radius after cutting successively along arbitrary axes is given by the closed form of a sum of squares of the remaining coordinates,

r_final² = R² − Σ x_k'².

This agrees strictly with the theorem of the author's earlier work (the composition operation of central projections, https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20060728 ). The r_final is no more than the radius of the sphere after the cut; it does not claim a physical or spacetime curvature.

## Two layers: what is fact and what is hypothesis

A stance common to both papers is to disclose honestly, splitting one's argument into two layers.

- First layer: established mathematics (the object of observation). The Robertson floor, the dimension series of division algebras, the closed form of the composition of central projections. These are merely cited, not proved.
- Second layer: the author's correspondence hypothesis. The part that connects that mathematics to a model particle with frequency as the basic quantity. Not a derivation from standard theory, but a working hypothesis.

The origin of the sign structure (from Euclidean to Minkowski, which axis acquires the minus sign) is not derived, and is explicitly set aside as a future task. The assignment of referents — which axis corresponds to mass, energy, or time — is undifferentiated in this system, which has no background, and is not committed to.

## What this series does not claim

- Any change to the predictions of standard quantum theory, general relativity, or special relativity.
- A physical derivation of the 3+1 spacetime dimension, the metric signature, or Minkowski structure.
- Identification of the fifth frequency ν₅ with rest mass (it observes only the coincidence of structure).
- A physical interpretation of projection and cut; prediction of new physical or cosmological constants; proof of any new mathematical theorem.

Evaluation and interpretation are left to the reader.
