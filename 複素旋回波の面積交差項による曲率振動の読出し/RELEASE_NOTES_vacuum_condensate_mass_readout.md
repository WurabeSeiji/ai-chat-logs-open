## Zenodo Metadata

**Keywords:** anonymous complex numbers, square-zero closure, standing-wave vacuum, localized condensate, center of mass, mass, Fourier-Mellin transform, characteristic function, gauge-invariant readout, emergent matter

**Description:** An anonymous Fourier-Mellin readout over the empirical measure of an anonymous complex standing-wave vacuum under square-zero closure detects localized condensates and defines their relative center of mass (first-order spectral phase), finite extent (second-order spectral decay), condensation weight (zeroth moment), and the distortion of the surrounding vacuum-state gauge, distinguishing residual-compensation-type from closure-preserving distortion. An appendix proves that constant wavelength-frequency product across all shell pairs is equivalent to P proportional to L^alpha, identifies alpha=1 as the null self-dual point of the previous paper, and constructs an exact rational-exponent shell family realizing spacelike/null/timelike readouts.
