# Reading Space, Time, and Matter out of a Collection of Complex Numbers

See comments for details

The starting point is a single closure condition, Σzₙ²=0. No meters, seconds, or kilograms are given from the start.
Complex states are not spacetime themselves. Only through a log-transformation readout do they appear as space and time.
In an exact special solution where infinitely many complex waves close, wavelength × frequency is constant and every interval is light-like.
The light-like structure is not forced by zero closure; it is selected by the self-duality condition α=1 between spatial and temporal scales.
Varying the exponent α, the three types spacelike, null, and timelike appear exactly within the same closure system.
When part of the homogeneous sea of waves condenses locally, a relative center of mass and a finite extent are born for the first time.
Condensation weight is the 0th moment, the center the 1st, the extent the 2nd — mass-like quantities read as a spectral moment hierarchy.
Matter may not be something placed inside the vacuum; it may be read as a localized condensation of the vacuum state itself.
Two mechanisms by which condensation distorts the surrounding vacuum are distinguished and fixed in a numerically testable form.
