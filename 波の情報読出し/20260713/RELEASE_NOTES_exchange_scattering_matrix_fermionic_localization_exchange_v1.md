## Summary

This release publishes a preliminary experiment on localization exchange in an AB closed phase system with an exchange-scattering-matrix fermionic collision map.

The experiment confirms that the acceleration-like harmonic readout remains available under the scattering-matrix rebound basis, that `N=1` is sufficient for the base readout floor, and that one-sided high odd harmonics can transfer localization through intermediate recursive scattering.

The result is limited to this model and does not claim a general derivation of wave-packet collapse.
