## Main Result

For a commutative extension field `K` containing the real field, the nontrivial algebraic quadratic-closure condition

```text
sum_k x_k^2 = 0,
x != 0
```

has no solution in `R^N`. In the minimal two-component case, `x^2 + y^2 = 0` with `x != 0` gives `(y/x)^2 = -1`; the minimal subfield generated by that element is `R(i)`, isomorphic to `C`. No complex conjugation is used in this derivation.

The paper distinguishes the conjugation-free closure form `Q_0 = sum x_k^2` from the positive-definite readout `Q_+ = sum x_k^* x_k`. With additional assumptions of continuity, reversibility, `Q_+` preservation, exchange symmetry, finite order, and squared readout, the preceding paper's discrete Born-type sequence is recovered.

## Connection Problem C2

The existing exchange kernel `U = P_s + zeta P_a` does not generally preserve the closure surface `Q_0=0`. For `x_0=(1,i)^T`,

```text
Q_0(U x_0) = i(1 - zeta^2).
```

Constructing a closure-preserving exchange map, a projection rule onto the closure surface, or an enlarged closure-preserving state space remains an explicit open problem.

## Scope and Limits

- Derived: nontrivial quadratic closure requires a nonreal direction.
- Additional construction: extension to continuous reversible phase rotation preserving `Q_+`.
- Conditional derivation: exchange symmetry, finite order, and squared readout yield discrete Born-type weights.
- Not derived: the complete Born rule, single-trial randomness, repeated-trial frequencies, unique conjugation, or unique unitarity.
