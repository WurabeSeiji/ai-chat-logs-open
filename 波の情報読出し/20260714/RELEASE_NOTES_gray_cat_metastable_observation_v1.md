## Summary

Released a preliminary numerical study on white-cat, black-cat, and gray-cat A/B allocation states in a closed phase system.

The study separates gray eigen, gray metastable, natural selection, and large-oscillation regimes in AB alone, then tests weak C readout and strong D observation.

## Main Result

```text
Gray metastable phase:
  weak C readout can read the gray allocation without one-sided selection;
  strong D observation can select white or black.

Gray eigen phase:
  strong D observation keeps the gray allocation.
```
