## Summary

This release closes the current acceleration-readout experiment series in the Wave Information Readout program by publishing two preliminary summary papers.

1. **Preliminary Summary of Harmonic Readout and c=1 Area Sweep in an AB Two-Body Closed Phase System**
2. **Preliminary Summary of Distance-Exponent Readout by Independent Metric C and Relational Compensation Decomposition in an ABC Closed Phase System**

The first paper shows that acceleration-like harmonic readout can be constructed from the label-free AB relation `f_AB` without introducing standard external forces, individual forces `f_A/f_B`, or a background space. It also shows that the AB two-body system alone lacks an independent gauge for determining whether the distance dependence is proportional, inverse, or inverse-square.

The second paper introduces the third wave `C` as an internal metric gauge and decomposes `f_AB`, `f_AC`, `f_BC`, and `f_ABC`. It confirms that the C gauge has a valid window, but no native inverse-law or inverse-square-law distance dependence appears in the present one-dimensional localized-wave harmonic model.

## Main Findings

### AB Summary

- `f_AB` can be read as a single label-free AB relation.
- Harmonic acceleration-like readout can be constructed without placing `f_A` or `f_B`.
- Protocol F/B degeneracy is confirmed under label-free readout.
- Readout-induced envelope decay disappears when the readout wave is stopped.
- Independent `tau_read` can form a `chi-tau` area.
- Constructed `1/A_chi_tau` can show `alpha≈2`, but native inverse-area scaling was not detected.
- The AB two-body system alone cannot independently determine the distance exponent.
- V4 confirms that direct A/B two-channel fermion-like recoil scattering does not break the label-free `D_AB`/`V_AB` readout or the independent-`tau_read` `chi-tau` surface.

### ABC Summary

- C gauge has a valid window.
- Too small `R_C` gives insufficient cell resolution.
- Too strong or biased C contaminates the AB readout through `f_AC`, `f_BC`, and `f_ABC`.
- Reading `tau_ABC` as representative time is supported.
- Separating `f_AB`, `f_AC`, `f_BC`, and `f_ABC` is supported.
- Directly adding `f_ABC` to the AB circumferential direction muddies classification.
- No native inverse-law or inverse-square-law distance dependence was detected in the present minimal model.

## Scope

This release does not claim a derivation of standard gravity, Coulomb force, or any universal inverse-square law.

The confirmed result is narrower and structural:

```text
Acceleration-like harmonic readout can be represented inside the closed phase system.
However, the present one-dimensional localized-wave harmonic model does not natively generate inverse-law or inverse-square distance dependence, even after adding the internal metric wave C.
```

The inverse-square question is therefore deferred to a separate line of work on observational statistics, area readout, localized-wave ensembles, and push-forward maps of initial fluctuations.
