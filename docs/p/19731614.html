Considerations on the Relationship between the 6-Dimensional Hypercube and Central Projection

6次元超直方体と中心投影との関係性の考察

Noriaki Kihara (WF System Co., Ltd.) · ORCID 0009-0004-6753-4020 · 2026-04-24 · Series: Wave Equation / Phase Equation & Delay-Circuit Model

Abstract

An investigation into the structural relationship between the 6-dimensional hypercube combinatorial framework and the central projection geometric framework. Three curvature radii R, R_0, R_1 are analyzed, connecting the discrete particle classification to the continuous spacetime geometry.
