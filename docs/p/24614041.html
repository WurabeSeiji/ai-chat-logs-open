Spontaneous Selection of Quaternionic and Octonionic Crystals, Cascade Melting, and Bimodal View-Angle Splitting under the Triangle-Loop Product on Cubic Arrays (Rapid Communication)

Noriaki Kihara (WF System Co., Ltd.) · ORCID 0009-0004-6753-4020 · 2026-11-28 · v1.0 · Series: Generative Structure of Dimensions

Abstract

This rapid communication reports exact integer, analytical, and deterministic numerical results for the triangle-loop product Tri(T)_{ijk} = sum_{p,q,r} T_{ipq} T_{qjr} T_{rpk} on N x N x N cubic arrays without background spacetime, external operators, randomness, or division.Spontaneous algebraic selection: Among Cayley–Dickson multiplication tables, only quaternions (N=4) and octonions (N=8) form exact period-2 crystals (U^2 = I); complex numbers (N=2) vanish at step 1 and sedenions (N=16) self-destruct at step 36 without defects.Exhaustive 1,024 single-cell defect spectrum on octonions: 0 zero extinctions (100% survival). Real zero-cell defects (448/448) precipitate into an 8-cell complex rotation-matrix pair; imaginary zero-cell defects split strictly 336:112 (3:1) into a 32-cell quaternionic intermediate phase and an 8-cell complex fixed point (both satisfying sum U^2 = 0 and Pauli projections I +/- sigma_y without external constraints).Exact analytical theorem & 20,000-step tracking: The real 8-cell survivor obeys z_j' = (z_0^2 + z_1^2) z_j, conserving z_1/z_0 while executing 3x-angle rotation on S^1 for >20,000 steps; the imaginary 32-cell phase undergoes a two-stage 8D -> 4D -> 2D cascade transition saturating by step 100 and invariant through step 20,000.Coupled large crystal N=64 (O_8 tensor O_8, 262,144 components): A real single-cell defect recrystallizes into a 512-cell composite survivor (stable through step 500, with a 3-level discrete energy hierarchy across 64 elements).Bimodal view-angle spectrum splitting: Coordinate-free triangle view-angle ratios rho = L_3/L_1 split sharply between background-connected triangles collapsing exponentially to causal separation (Delta ln > 687) and particle-internal triangles rebounding in a V-shape back to equilateral geometry (rho ~ 1), synchronized with energy condensation E_512 -> 1.Includes Japanese and English manuscripts (Markdown, LaTeX, PDF), build_tex.py, SHA256SUMS, and figures_and_programs.zip containing all 18 deterministic Python scripts, raw CSV/NPZ/TXT data, and Japanese/English figures. Includes a rigorous comparison with V. Chandrasekaran (arXiv:2611.34924v1).
