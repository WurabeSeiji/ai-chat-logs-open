The Second Thought Experiment: Deriving the Coulomb-Type Inverse-Square Force from a Product Readout and an Area Clock - Keeping Two Values and a Linear Law, Obtaining the Focus, Attraction, Repulsion and Neutrality, and Fixing What Two Values Cannot Write

第二思考実験：クーロン型の逆二乗の力を、積の読み出しと面積の時計から導く

Noriaki Kihara (WF System Co., Ltd.) · ORCID 0009-0004-6753-4020 · 2026-09-21 · v1.0 · Series: Generative Structure of Dimensions

Abstract

The law of the first thought experiment is kept unchanged: two values X=(a,b), conservation of an area readout, and the undirected second-order rule X_{k+1}+X_{k-1}=tau X_k. Only two design assumptions are added, both obtained by applying the principles of the design document to the readout side: position is also a product readout (D5), and the clock is fixed by imposing the conserved-readout axiom on the readout plane (D6). From P2 (a state alone is unreadable) applied to position, together with reading a product as a bilinear form, position must be read from the pair of a state and a relation. The operations tied to namelessness - the swap P and a second reflection D - generate a group whose three non-identity elements give exactly a basis of the space of quadratic readouts, so the readouts are x=w(X,PX)=a^2-b^2, y=w(X,DX)=2ab and r=w(X,J0 X)=a^2+b^2, with the identity x^2+y^2-r^2=0. The unique readout invariant under both P and D is r up to a constant, so the distance need not be chosen: it appears as a zero closure. The area swept in one step equals exactly q(X_k . X_{k+1}), so the tick is Delta t_k proportional to X_k . X_{k+1} and, in the continuum, dt proportional to r ds. It is then derived that: (1) the readout point z=x+iy obeys an inverse-square force with the origin as focus, d^2z/dt^2 = -mu z/r^3, exactly along the continuous interpolation and to second order in the step size for the discrete readout; (2) the orbit is a conic with the origin as focus, and its equation is the conserved quantity q of the first thought experiment written as a linear form in the readouts, with e^2 = 1 - 4 det G/(tr G)^2 - tau fixes only the type, while the value of e also depends on tr G; (3) the sign of the coupling is fixed from inside: in the hyperbolic case mu = -8 alpha beta cos psi, where psi is the opening angle of the sector the configuration lies in, so attraction and repulsion appear within one linear law as a difference of sectors, and the elliptic case is always attractive; (4) neutrality has a place from the start: for hyperbolic laws, orthogonality of the two null directions, symmetry of S, tr G = 0, and mu = 0 for every orbit are equivalent; (5) the identity mu = 2 q tr(Omega A) holds, from which the agreement of the two eccentricity formulas is an identity rather than a numerical coincidence, and from which it follows that zero angular momentum forces zero coupling - within two values and an invertible linear law, a collision orbit with L=0 and mu nonzero cannot be represented, so the map z=w^2 and the clock have the same form as the Levi-Civita regularization while the collision orbits that regularization treats lie outside the model; (6) what two values cannot write is fixed: the map (direction), because the shape freedom 2N-4 of N planar bodies vanishes at N=2, and the mass and charge ratios, because a single relation yields only the one combination mu_12. It is also shown that replacing D by any other reflection (other than plus or minus P) changes nothing: the choice of the orientation of the second reflection amounts to a convention of taking orthogonal coordinates for position, while the existence of a second reflection is the content of D5. Verification used 16 preregistered failure conditions plus 2 added afterwards; one preregistered item (E4c) actually failed and its diagnosis is reported without alteration. All appendix identities are checked by an included SymPy script (110 lines, all zero), and the verdicts were reproduced in a second independent environment. Japanese full text with English abstract; the reproduction bundle (scripts, JSON results, figures) is included.
