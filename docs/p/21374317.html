Preliminary Summary of Harmonic Readout and c=1 Area Sweep in an AB Two-Body Closed Phase System

AB二体閉鎖位相系における調和読出しとc=1面積スイープ予備実験総括 v4

Noriaki Kihara (WF System Co., Ltd.) · ORCID 0009-0004-6753-4020 · 2026-07-15 · v4 · Series: Information Readout of Waves (Closed Two-Channel Systems)

Abstract

This V4 release keeps the AB two-body harmonic readout and c=1 area sweep claims unchanged while replacing the previous recoil-factor implementation with a direct A/B two-channel scattering-matrix action on the incident channels. The result confirms that the acceleration-like harmonic readout and chi-tau area readout remain evaluable, and that label-free D_AB and V_AB remain degenerate with the transmission-type readout in the AB two-body closed phase system. The paper still does not claim a native inverse-area, inverse-law, or inverse-square-law derivation.
