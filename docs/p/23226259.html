The Twelfth Thought Experiment: From the Fine-Structure Constant to Observation Maps and Gauge Symmetry - Starting from the Fine-Structure Constant, Rereading Spacetime, Mass, Charge, Color Charge and Gauge Symmetry from the Light-Cone Equation and the Choice of Observable Axes

第十二思考実験：微細構造定数から観測写像とゲージ対称性へ

Noriaki Kihara (WF System Co., Ltd.) · ORCID 0009-0004-6753-4020 · 2026-10-08 · v1.0 · Series: Generative Structure of Dimensions

Abstract

The eleventh thought experiment (10.5281/zenodo.23199299) used the fine-structure constant as a plain input value. This note asks where it comes from. In the Bohr ground state of hydrogen alpha = v/c, so alpha is first a velocity ratio. The Lorentz factor gamma = 1/sqrt(1 - alpha^2) is only a component ratio of r^2 + tau^2 + (it)^2 = 0, the light-cone equation with one proper-time axis added: a worldline slower than light lies on the light cone once one axis is added. The same right triangle is the central projection l^2 = |x|^2 + R_c^2, where the curvature radius in the projection direction is the square root of the sum of squares of the not directly readable directions, R_c = sqrt(R^2 + Q1^2 + Q2^2 + Q3^2), and t corresponds to l. Extending the light cone to eight unnamed directions (x, y, z, t, R, Q1, Q2, Q3), an observer that reads a subspace sees the hidden directions only as a sum of squares; a transformation G with P(U) = P(GU) is an observational symmetry, so symmetry is read as indistinguishability. An observer reading only t sees an O(7)-type symmetry, and a 3+2 split of the five hidden directions gives S(U(3)xU(2)) = (SU(3)xSU(2)xU(1))/Z6, the same structure as the 3+2 split of SU(5) grand unification. Prior work (Minkowski, Epstein, Montanus, Kaluza-Klein, Wesson, Witten, Georgi-Glashow, Baez-Huerta, Furey, Rovelli) is cited. This is a conceptual note without computation; the eight dimensions, the signature and the 3+2 split are put in by hand. Japanese and English full texts and three reproducible figures (SVG/PNG with the generating script) are included.
