The EPR Paradox Is Not a Paradox: A Supplement to "Observation as Simultaneous Emission and Absorption" — Reading the Position–Momentum Pair as a Closure Identity

EPR パラドックスはパラドックスではない ― 「観測は放射と吸収の同時成立である」の補遺：位置と運動量の対を閉包の恒等式として読む ―

Noriaki Kihara (WF System Co., Ltd.) · ORCID 0009-0004-6753-4020 · 2026-11-01 · v1 · Series: Generative Structure of Dimensions

Abstract

The EPR argument has three steps. (i) Measuring the position of one member A of a pair predicts with certainty the position of the other member B, and measuring the momentum predicts B's momentum (perfect correlation). (ii) The measurement at A does not disturb the distant B (locality). (iii) Hence B's position and momentum are simultaneously elements of reality, and the quantum-mechanical description is incomplete. This paper takes the readout rule of the preceding paper "Observation as Simultaneous Emission and Absorption" as its premise and re-reads the argument. The pair is a closure q_A + q_B = 0 that held once at the source, from which x_B = −x_A and p_B = −p_A follow simultaneously as identities. A single end has no value (the one-sided distribution is uniform), nothing happens at B when A is read, and the correlation depends neither on the order of the readouts nor on the distance between the two. The element of reality is the relation called the closure, not an attribute of one end. Step (ii) is correct, step (i) is a relational identity, and only the attribution in step (iii) is mistaken. That a destructive readout exists, drops the chosen variable to one of two values and severs the relation with the conjugate variable, is placed as a known fact (resonant absorption, photodetection, homodyne detection); deriving its mechanism is a separate task. "Instantaneous selection" is not written: what can be shown is only that no finite-speed influence exists. No new number appears. Given the preceding paper, EPR is neither a paradox nor a mystery but a misreading of a relational identity. The Japanese version is the authoritative text; the English version is a translation.
