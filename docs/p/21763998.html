Structure of Counting Readouts in an Anonymous Two-Channel Closed Wave System

無名二チャネル閉鎖波系における数え上げ読出しの構造

Noriaki Kihara (WF System Co., Ltd.) · ORCID 0009-0004-6753-4020 · 2026-08-02 · v1 · Series: Generative Structure of Dimensions

Abstract

Part II of a trilogy. Establishes the structure of counting readouts in an anonymous two-channel closed wave system: exact rational equal-weight points, a meta counting rule with predictions fixed before measurement (20/20), a five-variant convention audit separating physics-grade from bookkeeping-grade statements, complete verification of the Niven intersection points (realized orders 3, 4, 6 = the complete nontrivial two-dimensional crystallographic menu), an integer-pair readout, and a refutation of the winding-number spectroscopy hypothesis. Japanese and English versions included.
