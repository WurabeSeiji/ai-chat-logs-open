QCD Expressed in Octonions ― Writing the Lagrangian of Quantum Chromodynamics As It Stands in Octonions, as a Reference for Checking Answers ―

QCD を八元数で表現したら ― 量子色力学のラグランジアンをそのまま八元数で書き、答え合わせの基準とする ―

Noriaki Kihara (WF System Co., Ltd.) · ORCID 0009-0004-6753-4020 · 2026-11-19 · v1.0 · Series: Generative Structure of Dimensions

Abstract

The author has been working toward the goal of reading out spacetime, particles, and interactions from the relations among the states of a finite complex Hilbert space, without placing a background spacetime. Quantum chromodynamics (QCD), however, had not been taken up in that work. Prompted by a public lecture by Professor Tetsuya Onogi (Science Night 2026, Graduate School of Science, The University of Osaka), in which the general form of the QCD Lagrangian was presented, the author considered whether this formula can be expressed in octonions as it stands.When one imaginary unit of the octonions is fixed, the octonions split into one complex number and three complex numbers, and the automorphisms of the octonions that do not move that unit form SU(3). This is the known construction of Günaydin and Gürsey (1973). This paper re-checks that construction with the author's multiplication table, and then (1) writes the QCD Lagrangian in the language of octonions, (2) expands the gluon-only term in octonion components and writes it down as seven conditions (15 − 7 = 8), (3) gives the correspondence with the reading of the octonions that the author has used so far, (t, xyz)(R, Q1 Q2 Q3), and (4) sorts what came out of the octonion side in this rewriting from what was still placed from outside.This paper contains no new theorem. It does not derive QCD from the octonions. Its purpose is to prepare a reference for checking answers when, in future work, the author examines whether spacetime, particles, and interactions can be read out from the relations among states expressed in octonions without placing a background. All formulas in the text were checked numerically with the attached program (25 checks).Files: Japanese and English manuscripts (md, tex, PDF), the verification program, and its output.
