Self-consistent Determination of the Curvature Radius and Recovery of the Tangherlini Evaporation Scaling (Paper 5)

曲率半径の自己整合的決定と Tangherlini 蒸発スケーリングの回復（論文5：動力学的接続）

Noriaki Kihara (WF System Co., Ltd.) · ORCID 0009-0004-6753-4020 · 2026-04-28 · v2 · Series: Black-Hole Thermodynamics Programme (incl. α-Identity)

Abstract

v2 (April 2026): Reference list refined per paper. External references corrected and finalized; no self-citations. v1 (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.19837596) remains the original publication.English: The curvature radius $R$ is determined self-consistently from matter content via $E_{total} \propto R^2$, giving $R \propto M^{1/2}$, $T_H \propto M^{-1/2}$, and lifetime $\tau \propto M^{3/2}$ — in agreement with the four-plus-one-dimensional Tangherlini result. The original apparent discrepancy in the evaporation rate exponent is shown to be a confusion between 3+1 and 4+1 conventions; in 4+1 dimensions the agreement is exact. The principal residual issue is the reduction to 3+1 dimensions.日本語: 曲率半径 $R$ を物質内容から $E_{total} \propto R^2$ により自己整合的に決定し、$R \propto M^{1/2}$, $T_H \propto M^{-1/2}$, 寿命 $\tau \propto M^{3/2}$ — 4+1次元 Tangherlini と一致 — を得る。蒸発レート指数の見かけの不一致は 3+1 と 4+1 規約の混同であり、4+1 次元では完全一致。残存問題は 3+1 次元への削減。
