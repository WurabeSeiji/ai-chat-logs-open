Linear Simplexification by Square-Quantity Readout and a Dimension-wise Organization of Curvature-Correction Candidates

平方量読出しによる線形単体化と曲率補正候補の次元別整理

Noriaki Kihara (WF System Co., Ltd.) · ORCID 0009-0004-6753-4020 · 2026-06-21 · v0.1-r1 · Series: Square-Quantity Readout Series

Abstract

An observational and organizing paper on the positive square map X_i=x_i^2, linear simplexification of sum-of-squares constraints, elementary motion-form readouts, and the dimension-wise appearance of curvature-correction candidates. The paper does not claim to derive physical laws; it juxtaposes square-quantity readout with the geodesic unit-cell area coefficient from the self-referenced Paper 0.
