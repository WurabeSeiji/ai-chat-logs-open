Abstract

v2 (April 2026): Reference list refined per paper. External references corrected and finalized; no self-citations. v1 (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.19837598) remains the original publication.English: We synthesize the results of the five companion papers and identify the principal open problem: how the 4+1-dimensional black hole thermodynamics established by the programme reduces to the observed 3+1-dimensional thermodynamics. We discuss three candidate mechanisms — Kaluza–Klein compactification, brane localization (Randall–Sundrum/ADD), and a holographic-style projection — and propose a programme of further investigation. No new theorems; the purpose is to crystallize what has and has not been established.日本語: 5姉妹論文の結果を統合し、主要未解決問題を同定する:プログラムが確立した4+1次元 BH 熱力学が観測 3+1 次元熱力学にどう削減するか。3つの候補機構(Kaluza–Klein コンパクト化、Randall–Sundrum/ADDブレーン局在化、ホログラフィー射影)を議論し、調査プログラムを提案する。新定理なし;目的は確立事項と未確立事項の明確化。
