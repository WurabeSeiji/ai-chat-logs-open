Emergence of a Three-Direction Space in an N-Body Relational-Wave Closed System: Generation of a Third Direction from a Two-Direction Initial State and a Quasi-Stable Closure

N体関係波閉鎖系における三方向空間の創発

Noriaki Kihara (WF System Co., Ltd.) · ORCID 0009-0004-6753-4020 · 2026-07-26 · v1.0 · Series: Generative Structure of Dimensions

Abstract

This paper tracks the long-time evolution of the new orthogonal rotation plane found in Paper 6 for an N-body relational-wave closed system. For N=5, 40, 300, a two-direction initial state generates an independent third space direction, and the combined rank of the initial parent plane and the instantaneous dominant plane increases from 2 to 4, forming a three-direction quasi-stable dynamical closure. Within the investigated time range (absolute step 0 to 55000) no additional finite-occupation direction beyond three appeared, while a positive transverse growth response persisted outside the three-direction closure. The record includes the full paper text, figures, and the complete reproduction programs and numerical results. (Version 1.0, Preprint.)
