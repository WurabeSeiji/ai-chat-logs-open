Curvature Renormalization and Perfect-Reflection Stability by Curved Closed Stationary Waves

曲率付き閉鎖定常波による曲率繰り込みと完全反射安定性 v2

Noriaki Kihara (WF System Co., Ltd.) · ORCID 0009-0004-6753-4020 · 2026-07-13 · v2 · Series: Information Readout of Waves (Closed Two-Channel Systems)

Abstract

This release publishes a numerical constructive experiment on how odd-harmonic complex waves satisfying the all-positive zero closure Sigma x_n^2 = 0 are stabilized inside curved local cells. Curvature-induced relative phase leakage is shown to produce closure residuals and transmission leakage in transient states, while internal phase re-selection into closed stationary waves absorbs the leakage and recovers the complete-reflection readout of the preceding local exchange-interference map. The release includes Japanese and English manuscripts, TeX/PDF builds, executed numerical scripts, result JSON/CSV files, figures, and a reproducibility bundle.
