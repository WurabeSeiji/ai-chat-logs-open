Formulation of Structural Requirements for a Theory of Everything

万物の理論が満たすべき構造要件の定式化

Noriaki Kihara (WF System Co., Ltd.) · ORCID 0009-0004-6753-4020 · 2026-04-16 · Series: Other / Not stored in this repository

Abstract

This paper formulates the structural requirements that a Theory of Everything must satisfy, based on rigorous definitions of information and operations. Starting from minimal axioms of information I and operation F*, it derives causal structure from cognitive accessibility and enumerates nine structural requirements including dimensional unification (all dimensions expressible as L^n) and scale symmetry (scale homogeneity of all equations).
