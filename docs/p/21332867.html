Interference Construction of a Perfect Reflection Map from a Fermionic Inverse-Phase Core

フェルミオン的逆相核による完全反射写像の干渉構成 v2

Noriaki Kihara (WF System Co., Ltd.) · ORCID 0009-0004-6753-4020 · 2026-07-13 · v2 · Series: Information Readout of Waves (Closed Two-Channel Systems)

Abstract

This release publishes an additional constructive experiment in which the direct direction-reversal rule used in a previous AB/C complete elastic-reflection simulation is replaced by a local exchange-interference map controlled by the internal phase of a fermionic inverse-phase core. The release includes Japanese and English manuscripts, TeX/PDF builds, the executed numerical script, result JSON/CSV files, figures, and a reproducibility bundle.
