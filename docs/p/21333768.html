Preliminary Summary of Acceleration-Basis and Localization Exchange in Exchange-Interference Scattering-Matrix Fermion-Like Collisions

交換干渉散乱行列フェルミオン的衝突における加速度基底と局在性交換予備実験総括 v1

Noriaki Kihara (WF System Co., Ltd.) · ORCID 0009-0004-6753-4020 · 2026-07-13 · v1 · Series: Information Readout of Waves (Closed Two-Channel Systems)

Abstract

This preliminary summary reports exchange-interference scattering-matrix experiments in a closed phase system. It evaluates whether an acceleration-like AB two-body readout is preserved while low odd-harmonic bottoms, one-sided high-harmonic conditions, recursive scattering, and reflection-rate sweeps are recorded as localization-index exchange in the outgoing channels.
