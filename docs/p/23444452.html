A Catalogue of Symmetries and Magic Numbers for Reading Out Spacetime from a State Space: The Answer-Check Standard of a Research Programme Taking Anonymity and Self-Consistency as Axioms

自己引用の対応表（2026-10-17）

Noriaki Kihara (WF System Co., Ltd.) · ORCID 0009-0004-6753-4020 · 2026-10-18 · v1.0 · Series: Generative Structure of Dimensions

Abstract

This paper fixes the answer-check standard of a research programme that seeks to read out time, space, mass, charge and interactions from the relations of a set of complex numbers {z_n} alone, without placing a background spacetime, a ruler or particle species by hand. It presents no new derivation. Instead it catalogues the symmetries the programme must satisfy and the dimensionless numbers (magic numbers) attached to them, giving every entry a status (established, theory, candidate, open) and a provenance (a published paper or external literature).The zeroth axiom is anonymity and the first axiom is self-consistency. A distinguishability axiom derived from anonymity states that the readout cannot tell which layer a value belongs to: the squared quantity z' = z^2 is also just a complex number. The single closure definition is the linear zero closure Σz_n = 0; the true-square closure Σz_n^2 = Σz'_n = 0 is the same definition read at the squared layer. The finite-order return U^n = I is a theorem. Below these sit big-picture symmetries, conjugacy and duality, the projection symmetry, and a candidate axiom that essential symmetries carry dimensionless magic numbers rather than Noether conserved quantities. The Standard Model, general relativity, E8, the algebra and geometry of the system, and the locking symmetries of complex systems are listed in the same format; magic numbers are split into integer, coupling-convention, irrational and derived systems.Two mathematical checks are included: the only linear maps preserving both the true square and the norm are real orthogonal maps; and the 24 Hurwitz units split into one vector and two spinor octets (8+8+8) that triality cycles as q -> a q a with a = (-1+i+j+k)/2, preserving the 240 roots of E8. All 156 published papers are mapped to the catalogue rows; axiom numbering is reset, an earlier lock-type claim on alpha is revised, and numerical disagreements are kept as open problems with multiple interpretations. The root difficulty of the state-space-to-spacetime map is placed in the dependence of what is readable on the projection method; since spacetime is stably readable, the map must be at least a partial projection.
