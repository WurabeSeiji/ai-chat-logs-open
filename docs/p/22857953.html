The First Thought Experiment: Tracing the Equivalence Principle Down to Two Nameless Degrees of Freedom - Deriving the Sign of the Sum of Squares, the Curvature Radius, and Three Systems (Inertial, Uniformly Accelerated, Rotating) from the Conservation of an Area Readout

第一思考実験：等価原理を無名な二自由度まで遡る

Noriaki Kihara (WF System Co., Ltd.) · ORCID 0009-0004-6753-4020 · 2026-09-20 · v1.0 · Series: Generative Structure of Dimensions

Abstract

Einstein's equivalence principle is traced down to a minimal system that presupposes no background spacetime, no metric, and no complex numbers. The inputs are two principles (two nameless degrees of freedom (a,b)~(b,a) as the minimal expression distinguishing state from relation; a state alone is unreadable and only products are readable), one axiom candidate (a conserved readout exists), and three design assumptions (real values, linearity, bilinearity). It is then derived that: (1) the readout of two configurations is uniquely, up to a constant, the area form w(X,Y)=a_X b_Y - b_X a_Y - Euclidean and Minkowski inner products assign values to a single configuration and violate the principle; (2) conservation of the signed readout is equivalent to det S=1, and the law becomes an undirected second-order rule X_{k+1}+X_{k-1}=tau X_k, in which the first readable dynamical quantity is the centripetal acceleration - the operational content of the equivalence principle appears as the structure of readout rather than as a postulate; (3) the sum of squares is not an input but is induced as Q_S(X)=w(X,SX), whose signature is set by tau (elliptic a^2+b^2, parabolic degenerate, hyperbolic a^2-b^2), so Sum z_n^2=C^2 holds in all three classes with z_2 real, nilpotent, or imaginary; (4) C equals exactly, at any step size, the curvature radius of the orbit in the metric induced by the interaction itself; (5) the three classes match the inertial, uniformly accelerated (Born hyperbolic motion, C=c^2/g, the 1907 clock effect), and rotating systems; (6) zero closure has two substantive forms - null cones and orbit sums Sum z_k^2=0 for n>=3; (7) the closure number n need not be postulated: under a finite resolution epsilon it is quantized to continued-fraction denominators of the rotation number, with n < 2 pi C / epsilon. The chain 'conservation of signed q (det S=1) implies undirected law implies conservation of |q| (|det S|=1)' is established, with counterexamples showing that neither converse holds; the orientation-reversing branch det S=-1 is recorded as unexplored. Verification uses 15 preregistered failure conditions plus 2 added afterwards; one preregistered item (E4b) actually failed, and its diagnosis is reported without alteration. All symbolic identities are checked by an included SymPy script, and the numerical verdicts were reproduced in a second independent environment (different OS, Python, NumPy, and BLAS). Japanese full text with English abstract; the reproduction bundle (scripts, JSON results, figures) is included.
