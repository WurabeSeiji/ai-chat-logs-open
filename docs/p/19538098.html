Schwarzschild–de Sitter Exact Solution in the Central Projection Framework

中心投影フレームワークにおける Schwarzschild–de Sitter 厳密解

Noriaki Kihara (WF System Co., Ltd.) · ORCID 0009-0004-6753-4020 · 2026-04-12 · Series: Central Projection Framework (Gnomonic Spacetime Geometry)

Abstract

We construct the Schwarzschild–de Sitter metric as the unique spherically symmetric static exact solution of the vacuum Einstein equation derived in Paper [1] of this series. The cosmological constant Lambda = 3/R^2 is intrinsically determined by the central projection framework, requiring no additional assumptions. We examine the R -> 0 limit and connect it to the nested structure of Paper [4].
