Abstract

This record contains the main Japanese and English papers, supporting axiom/specification/result documents, TeX/PDF outputs, and reproducibility bundle for a numerical constructive experiment on finite-resolution elastic reflection of two identifiable fermionic local waves in a closed phase system without assuming an external background space.
