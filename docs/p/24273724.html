Projection Axis Selection and Covariance of Conservation Laws ― Two Radii Carried by All Waves, Projection Asymmetry of 1-, 2-, and 4-Dimensional Subalgebras, and Reduction to Minimal Systems ―

投影軸の選択と保存則の共変性 ― 全波が乗る「2つの R」、1, 2, 4 次元部分多元体の投射非対称性、および最小系への縮約原理 ―

Noriaki Kihara (WF System Co., Ltd.) · ORCID 0009-0004-6753-4020 · 2026-11-16 · v1.0 · Series: Generative Structure of Dimensions

Abstract

This manuscript proposes and predicts a unified principle—projection axis selection and covariance of conservation laws—in response to the open question left in the preceding thought-experiment paper (Concept DOI 10.5281/zenodo.24267347): how spacetime and internal spaces separate and reconstruct from a bounded Hilbert space without assuming a background spacetime. Connecting the axis-selection symmetry of the null condition sum(x_n^2) = 0 and the three-layer model R–R1–R0 to the algebraic structure of quaternions and octonions, we advance four claims and predictions: (1) all waves in the Hilbert space simultaneously reside on both the projection-axis side (R0) and the spatial side (R1); (2) by Hurwitz's theorem, choosing a multiplicatively closed subalgebra as R0 restricts the axis dimension to 1, 2, or 4 (R, C, H), switching the emergent space/symmetry between S^2, C^3/SU(3), and S^4; (3) conservation laws are covariant under projection-axis re-selection; and (4) conservation of R'^2 in the minimal system x^2 + y^2 + z^2 = R'^2 is inherited in higher dimensions as a composite conservation law R'^2 = t^2 + R^2 + Q1^2 + Q2^2 + Q3^2 over all compressed dimensions.
