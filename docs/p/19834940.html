Diameter of the Circumscribed Hypersphere of a Unit Four-Dimensional Hyperrectangle — Spacetime Dimensionality, the Origin of Time, and the Beginning of the Universe as Derived from a Discrete Packing Model

体積1の四次元超直方体に外接する超球体の直径は？

Noriaki Kihara (WF System Co., Ltd.) · ORCID 0009-0004-6753-4020 · 2026-04-28 · v2 · Series: Central Projection Framework (Gnomonic Spacetime Geometry)

Abstract

Rev.2 (April 2026): The §4 packing argument has been reformulated as dense packing of unit cubes into a four-dimensional ball of radius R = 2k+1, defining a number-theoretic sequence N(k) (1, 137, 1545, 7281, ...) whose packing density asymptotically converges to 1 as k → ∞. The original v1 (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.19533313) had a constant packing density of 2/π² ≈ 0.2026 because it merely placed an inscribed hypercube inside a hypersphere without filling the corner gaps. Sections §7–§20 (signed volume, time-axis origin, cosmological metaphor) are preserved with (2k+1)^4 → N(k) substitution. New §5 derives the Lagrange–Jacobi number-theoretic background. / 改訂版 (2026年4月): §4の充填論証を「半径R=2k+1の4次元球への単位立方体の稠密充填」として再定式化し、充填率がk→∞で1に漸近収束する数論的数列N(k) (1, 137, 1545, 7281, ...) を導出。初版v1は外接球の隙間を埋めず充填率が常に2/π²≈0.2026で頭打ちだった。§7以降は (2k+1)^4 → N(k) の置換で論証構造を保持。新§5でLagrange–Jacobi定理の数論的背景を導出。English: A purely mathematical thought experiment. Starting from two assumptions — discreteness and the existence of stable minimal units — and the principle of volume conservation, we deductively derive: (1) n = 4 as the minimal non-trivial dimension where √n is an integer; (2) a 1-versus-3 asymmetry from single-axis reversal of signed volume, structurally analogous to the Minkowski signature (−,+,+,+); (3) the possibility of expansion from a zero-volume initial state without violating conservation; (4) time as an emergent order from statistical alignment of reversal axes. All cosmological language (Big Bang, matter, antimatter, etc.) is metaphorical. This paper makes no physical claims, no empirical predictions, and no assertions about the actual universe.日本語: 純粋に数学的な思考実験。離散性と安定な最小単位の存在という2つの仮定、および体積保存則から、(1) √nが整数となる最小の非自明次元としてn=4、(2) 符号付き体積の1軸反転によるミンコフスキー計量の符号構造(−,+,+,+)との構造的類似、(3) 体積ゼロからの保存則を破らない展開の可能性、(4) 反転軸の統計的整列としての時間の創発を演繹的に導出する。宇宙論的用語(ビッグバン、物質、反物質等)はすべて比喩である。物理的主張・実証的予言・現実の宇宙に関する断言は一切含まない。
