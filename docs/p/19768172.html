Geometric Structure of Generation Mixing: Qualitative Derivation of CKM and PMNS Matrices

世代混合の幾何学的構造——CKM行列とPMNS行列の定性的導出

Noriaki Kihara (WF System Co., Ltd.) · ORCID 0009-0004-6753-4020 · 2026-04-26 · Series: Other / Not stored in this repository

Abstract

A structural answer to the generation mixing problem posed in the companion paper on 6-dimensional hypercube particle classification (§9.11.6). The S_3 permutation symmetry of three generations and its breaking pattern qualitatively reproduce the hierarchical structure of the CKM and PMNS mixing matrices.
