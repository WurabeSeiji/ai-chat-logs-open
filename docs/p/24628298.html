Two-Particle Dynamical Scattering, Gauge-Mediated Interactions, and Dynamical CHSH Bell Violation in the 3D Triangle-Loop Product (Part VI)

三次元正方配列の三角形ループ積における 2 粒子動的散乱、ゲージ媒介相互作用、および空間分離 CHSH ベル不等式の動的破れ（Part VI）

Noriaki Kihara (WF System Co., Ltd.) · ORCID 0009-0004-6753-4020 · 2026-11-28 · v1.0 · Series: Generative Structure of Dimensions

Abstract

Part VI following Parts I–V (Concept DOIs 10.5281/zenodo.24622200, 24622565, 24625962, 24626592, 24627519). On the full dense H = O_8 tensor M_8 (N=64) dynamics without random numbers, we establish: (1) two-body gravitational collapse lowering epsilon_c from 0.273580 to 0.269267 (ratio 0.9842, sign-independent); (2) exact local U(1) gauge redundancy (0.000e+00 difference without links) vs. like/opposite charge splitting via shared U(1) gauge links; (3) causal remote spin-2 (sigma_x) scattering (0.0000 rad at tau=1, exact quadratic arrival -1.4940 epsilon_s2^2 at tau=2, up to -2.289 rad at tau=8); (4) vacuum Tsirelson saturation S_CHSH = 2.828358 (~2 sqrt(2)) on all spatially separated site pairs and dynamical self-healing from product states (2.1462 -> 2.8259 in 3 steps); and (5) discontinuous entanglement collapse (2 sqrt(2) -> 0, purity 1.0 -> 0.25) at the event horizon threshold epsilon_c ~ 0.2736.
