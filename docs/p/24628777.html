Abstract

Part VII following Parts I–VI (Concept DOIs 10.5281/zenodo.24622200, 24622565, 24625962, 24626592, 24627519, 24628297). Deterministically verifies without random numbers: (1) the off-diagonal three-generation cyclic product J_off (24D) of J_3(O) is an exact zero-error eigen-crystal of Tri(T) with eigenvalue -1/64 (residual 0.000e+00); (2) SVD null-space extraction of F_4 (52D) > SO(8) (28D) > SO(7)_1 (21D, fixed-vector dims [1,0,0] proving 8_v + 8_s + 8_c triality) > G_2 (14D) > SU(3)_C (8D) with identical Casimir eigenvalues [0,0,8/9x6] across all three generations; (3) nonlinear 2-to-3-order hierarchy amplification on J_off while clarifying that Koide Q does not universally lock to 2/3; and (4) parameter-free bare gauge unification alpha_3:alpha_2:alpha_1 = 1:1:1 and sin^2(theta_W) = alpha_em/alpha_s = 3/8 on 64D, explicitly separating the low-energy RG running boundary.
