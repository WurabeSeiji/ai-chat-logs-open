On Dimension Addition to Background Spaces and Subjective Spaces

背景空間と主観空間への次元追加についての考察

Noriaki Kihara (WF System Co., Ltd.) · ORCID 0009-0004-6753-4020 · 2026-04-12 · Series: Central Projection Framework (Gnomonic Spacetime Geometry)

Abstract

Building on the central projection framework and the nested structure existence proposition, we examine the addition of new dimensions to background spaces and finite-curvature subjective spaces. Dimension addition is classified into two types depending on whether the new dimension is governed by the existing curvature radius R (Type I) or introduces an independent curvature radius R' (Type II). Structural constraints on each type are described as logical conditions arising from interpretive consistency. No physical claims are made.
