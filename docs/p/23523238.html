Relational Readout of Quaternions and Crystallization as Inflation: Anonymity-Preserving Autonomous Structuring and Partial Projection

四元数の関係読み出しと、結晶化としてのインフレーション

Noriaki Kihara (WF System Co., Ltd.) · ORCID 0009-0004-6753-4020 · 2026-10-21 · v1.1 · Series: Generative Structure of Dimensions

Abstract

Hypothesis paper (Japanese original with an English translation). The author's research programme aims to read out spacetime and particles from the relations within an anonymous set of complex numbers {z_n}, without placing a background spacetime, a ruler, or particle species from outside, and to connect this to the fact that general relativity and the Standard Model hold locally with extremely high accuracy. The previous paper (Concept DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23451494) built the map {z_n} → {q_n} → (1+3) as a partial projection, but left open how structure can arise autonomously in an anonymous set of quaternions without supplying from outside which quaternion relates to which. This paper proposes that the mechanism is crystallization, as in condensed-matter physics. From the relation between two states, a readout with the visual angle as distance is defined. For two equal-amplitude complex numbers, the coupling coefficient g = |z1 − z2|/|z1 + z2| = tan(Δθ/2) and the angular-size distance D = 1/g = cot(Δθ/2): in phase is at infinity (no action), antiphase is contact (total reflection). For quaternions the coupling coefficient k = (q1 + q2)^−1 (q1 − q2) = −n tan(α/2) becomes a three-dimensional vector. k is the stereographic projection of the relative phase u = q1^−1 q2 seen from the observer, and also the central projection of the square root of the relative phase; coupling strength 1 (orthogonality) corresponds to the light cone of the previous paper. Because the readout preserves angles, a localized observer cannot distinguish uniform curvature, global scale, the choice of map, or the choice of left/right frame. Local flatness is a property of the readout, not an approximation; only direction-dependent distortion (tides) is observable. Connectivity is not given from outside: the convex hull of an unlabelled point set on S³ gives a tetrahedral truss, which becomes a Delaunay triangulation under observer-centred stereographic projection (a conformally invariant test). Non-uniqueness of the triangulation is taken as a principle. With the rigidity count F − S = 3V − 6 − E, only partial projection, between zero projection (no structure) and full projection (rigid crystal), combines a skeleton with room for rearrangement. Since the structure is not unique, composition requires a process. When a seed enters a supercooled, highly symmetric state, the number of bondable faces multiplies by r (r ≤ 3) per process, giving geometric crystallization; with a ∝ V^(1/3), the number of e-folds is N_e = (n/3) ln r, and r = 3 needs n ≈ 164 processes for 60 e-folds. The end is given structurally as faces close and r → 1. Regular tetrahedra cannot fill flat space (gap 7.36°), but the 600-cell fills S³, and its 120 vertices are unit quaternions. A locally flat readout cannot close completely, so defect lines necessarily remain and partial projection is enforced without external parameters; the deficit angles carry curvature. The author's spacetime equation x² + y² + z² + t² = R² + Q1² + Q2² + Q3² is the null condition of split octonions and is equivalent to a pair of equal-amplitude quaternions (spacetime side and internal side). Verification plan (V1–V5) and falsification conditions are given in §13.
