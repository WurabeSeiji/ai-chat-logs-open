Relational Readout of Quaternions and Crystallization as Inflation: Anonymity-Preserving Autonomous Structuring and Partial Projection

四元数の関係読み出しと、結晶化としてのインフレーション ― 無名性を保った自律的構造化と部分投射 ―

Noriaki Kihara (WF System Co., Ltd.) · ORCID 0009-0004-6753-4020 · 2026-10-21 · v1.1 · Series: Generative Structure of Dimensions

Abstract

An anonymous set of quaternions has no structure by itself; this hypothesis paper proposes that structure arises autonomously, without supplying connectivity from outside, as crystallization in the sense of condensed-matter physics. From the relation of two equal-amplitude complex numbers, the coupling coefficient g = tan(Δθ/2) and the angular-size distance D = cot(Δθ/2) are read out (in phase is at infinity, antiphase is contact). For quaternions the coupling coefficient k = (q₁ + q₂)⁻¹(q₁ − q₂) = −n tan(α/2) is a three-dimensional vector; it is the stereographic projection of the relative phase and the central projection of its square root, and coupling strength 1 corresponds to the light cone. Since the readout preserves angles, a localized observer cannot distinguish uniform curvature, scale, map or left/right frame: local flatness is a property of the readout and only tides are visible. Connectivity is read out from the convex hull of anonymous points on S³, which becomes a Delaunay triangulation under observer-centred stereographic projection. With the rigidity count F − S = 3V − 6 − E, only partial projection combines a skeleton with room for rearrangement, and composition requires a process. A seed in a supercooled, highly symmetric state gives geometric crystallization with factor r ≤ 3 per process, N_e = (n/3) ln r (n ≈ 164 for 60 e-folds at r = 3), ending as faces close. Regular tetrahedra cannot fill flat space (gap 7.36°) but the 600-cell fills S³, so defect lines necessarily remain in a locally flat readout and partial projection is enforced without external parameters. The spacetime equation x² + y² + z² + t² = R² + Q₁² + Q₂² + Q₃² is the null condition of split octonions, equivalent to equal amplitude of the spacetime-side and internal-side quaternions. A verification plan and falsification conditions are given.
