Radial Projection: Definition and Relation to Central Projection

本ノートの性格

Noriaki Kihara (WF System Co., Ltd.) · ORCID 0009-0004-6753-4020 · 2026-06-06 · v3.3 · Series: Central Projection Framework (Gnomonic Spacetime Geometry)

Abstract

Technical Note. We define the radial projection sigma_R(x) = (R/||x||) x from R^(n+1) minus origin to S^n(R) and establish its basic properties (smoothness, idempotence, quotient structure, kernel of differential, angle preservation, scale invariance). We show that the central projection Phi_R of the authors prior works coincides with sigma_R restricted to the tangent hyperplane Pi_R = {x : x_(n+1) = R}, with image equal to the open upper hemisphere S^n_+(R) where Phi_R is a diffeomorphism. The contrast between the non-injective sigma_R (radial fibers collapsed) and the injective Phi_R (full geometric structure) clarifies what is gained and lost in extending the domain. This note serves as a foundational reference for the central projection series.
