Full Spherical Coverage of Central Projection in Discrete Space and the Number-Theoretic Necessity of 5-Dimensional Background Space

離散空間における中心投影の全球被覆と5次元背景空間の整数論的必然性

Noriaki Kihara (WF System Co., Ltd.) · ORCID 0009-0004-6753-4020 · 2026-04-17 · Series: Wave Equation / Phase Equation & Delay-Circuit Model

Abstract

This paper derives, from fundamental theorems of number theory, that 4 is the minimum dimension for an isotropic discrete lattice (Lagrange four-square theorem), that 5 dimensions are required as the background space for central projection (homogeneous coordinates), and constructs a 5-axis 10-face relay model that resolves the equatorial divergence and south-hemisphere invisibility problems of inverse mapping on S⁴(R).
