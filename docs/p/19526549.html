On the Limits of the Curvature Radius in Central Projection — The Cases R → ∞ and R → 0

中心投影における曲率半径の極限についての考察

Noriaki Kihara (WF System Co., Ltd.) · ORCID 0009-0004-6753-4020 · 2026-04-12 · Series: Central Projection Framework (Gnomonic Spacetime Geometry)

Abstract

central projection framework established in prior papers [1]–[3]. Both limits R → ∞ and R → 0 are geometrically regular. For R → 0, a structural tension arises between the geodesic deviation upper bound and local curvature. A nested structure of subjective spaces (Proposition 4.1) is shown to be constructible from existing geometric degrees of freedom, resolving this tension within the framework. No physical claims are made.
