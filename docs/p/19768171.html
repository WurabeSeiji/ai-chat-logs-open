Formulation of Signed Area: Derivation of Charge Structure and Consequences of Spin-2 Configurations

符号付き面積の定式化——電荷構造の導出とスピン2配置の帰結

Noriaki Kihara (WF System Co., Ltd.) · ORCID 0009-0004-6753-4020 · 2026-04-26 · Series: Other / Not stored in this repository

Abstract

Three consequences derived from the sign product P defined in the companion paper on 6-dimensional hypercube particle classification (§7): (1) derivation of electric charge from signed area, (2) weak isospin I_3 from piecewise definition, and (3) spin-2 graviton uniqueness from sign constraints.
