An Information-Theoretic Framework for Information Transmission / 情報伝達の情報論的整理

情報伝達の情報論的整理

Noriaki Kihara (WF System Co., Ltd.) · ORCID 0009-0004-6753-4020 · 2026-04-12 · Series: Wave Equation / Phase Equation & Delay-Circuit Model

Abstract

Three properties are defined for information I: the null state, equivalence judgment, and inversion. Two types of operational elements are defined: the delay circuit F_D and the NOT operation circuit. By combining these elements, discrete oscillation patterns and propagation patterns are constructed. / 情報Iに3つの性質(null状態、等価判定、反転)を定義し、ディレイ回路F_DとNOT演算回路の2種類の演算要素を定義。振動・伝播パターンを構成する。
