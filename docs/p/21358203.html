Preliminary Numerical Study of Gray-Cat Metastable Interfaces, Weak C Readout, and Strong D Observation Selection in a Closed Phase System

白猫黒猫灰色猫準安定界面におけるC弱読出しとD強観測選択予備実験総括 v1

Noriaki Kihara (WF System Co., Ltd.) · ORCID 0009-0004-6753-4020 · 2026-07-14 · v2 · Series: Information Readout of Waves (Closed Two-Channel Systems)

Abstract

This preliminary numerical study models white-cat and black-cat alternatives as internal A/B allocation amplitudes in a closed complex phase system. It distinguishes a gray-cat eigen phase, a gray-cat metastable phase, natural selection, and large oscillation regimes. It then tests weak C readout windows and strong D observation selection while comparing against no-D controls. The study shows that weak readout can preserve and read a metastable gray allocation, while stronger observation can select a white or black allocation; gray eigen states remain gray even under strong observation.
