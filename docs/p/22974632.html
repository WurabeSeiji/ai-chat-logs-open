The Sixth Thought Experiment: Can Two Kinds of Long-Range Interaction Be Internalized into a Single Closed State Map? - Reconstructing Gravity, Coulomb, GW-Quadrupole and EM-Dipole Radiation of a Charged Quasi-Circular Binary with Eight Persistent States, and Verifying the Transferability of One Transition over Five Conditions

第六思考実験：二種類の長距離相互作用を一つの閉じた状態写像へ内部化できるか

Noriaki Kihara (WF System Co., Ltd.) · ORCID 0009-0004-6753-4020 · 2026-09-26 · v1.1 · Series: Generative Structure of Dimensions

Abstract

The fifth thought experiment (10.5281/zenodo.22973086) self-audited, from the viewpoint of information flow, the gravitational-orbit generator that the fourth thought experiment had displayed as two states, and confirmed that in the present representation the information held across macro steps must be made explicit as Z_6 = (U, P, E, H, Q, N). The RK4 intermediate quantities and the processing phase were placed inside the state as machine/work states, and the generator was reconstructed as an OLD-state-only synchronous update with no external physical parameters, a non-feedback readout and a single transition(z) as the only generating path. This paper applies those audit rules, for the first time, to a system extended from one kind of interaction to two. The object is a charged two-body system restricted to G = M = c = 1, non-spinning point particles, quasi-circular orbits and a leading-order adiabatic inspiral: the conservative motion uses Newtonian gravity and the Coulomb interaction, and the dissipation uses the leading gravitational quadrupole radiation and the leading electric-dipole radiation. On the independent analytic reference side, the charge dependence reduces to two relational quantities, C = 1 - lambda_A lambda_B and D = (lambda_A - lambda_B)^2 with lambda = q/m: C enters the conservative force and the orbital angular velocity, and D decides the presence and strength of the leading electric-dipole radiation. Adding C and D to the six persistent states of the fifth thought experiment gives Z_8 = (U, P, E, H, Q, N, C, D). The implementation uses a 41-real-component full microstate consisting of these eight persistent logical states, the RK4 work registers and the 11-phase one-hot phase; transition(z) takes no external physical argument, builds every candidate value from the OLD full state only, and generates the next state simultaneously by a one-hot sum of products. N, C and D are held as identity candidates in every phase, so they are preserved as a result of the same map rather than by an external copy. For the reference case lambda_A = +0.30, lambda_B = -0.30 (C = 1.09, D = 0.36, N = nu = 0.25), the strict eight-state generation from r = 50 to 20 completed in 488,345 macro steps; the differences from the analytic reference were |dt| = 1.46e-8, |dphi| = 8.44e-11 and max |dr| = 9.12e-9 over the whole stored range, with dN = dC = dD = 0. With the same transition(z), the same 11-phase machine and the same eight persistent states unchanged, five conditions differing only in the initial C and D - (+0.30,-0.30), (+0.30,+0.30), (+0.90,-0.90), (+0.90,+0.90), (+1.20,-1.20) - were run; in all five the orbital state generation completed and the drift of N, C, D was 0. For equal-sign equal-ratio cases D = 0, so only the leading electric-dipole term adopted here vanishes while the Coulomb repulsion effect with C < 1 remains in the conservative motion. The n3_repulsive case with C = 0.19 required about 3816.7 orbits; P and H were generated to the end, but the time readout through the exponential clock Q exceeded the finite-precision range, which is recorded as it is as a limit of the domain and of the numerical representation. What this paper shows is that, for the restricted leading-order quasi-circular charged binary, the conservative effects of gravity and Coulomb and the dissipative effects of GW quadrupole and EM dipole radiation can be rearranged into a single synchronous state map with no external charge parameters and no branching by force type. It does not mean a fundamental unification of gravity and electromagnetism, a derivation of the Einstein-Maxwell equations, the minimality of eight states, the ontological fundamentality of C and D, or universality for general charged binaries.
