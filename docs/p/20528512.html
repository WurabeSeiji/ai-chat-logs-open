A Thought Experiment on a Model Particle — From the Phase-Area Floor to the Admissibility Condition for Dimension

あるモデル粒子をめぐる思考実験——位相面積の床から次元の許容条件へ

Noriaki Kihara (WF System Co., Ltd.) · ORCID 0009-0004-6753-4020 · 2026-06-03 · Series: Black-Hole Thermodynamics Programme (incl. α-Identity)

Abstract

An observational paper in the form of a thought experiment. It modifies no prediction of standard quantum theory and proves no new theorem. Through a single abstract model particle carrying one internal phase plane, it lays side by side, under the single lens of phase-space area, two established pieces of mathematics: the Robertson uncertainty floor (the absence of zero-area point-like states in phase space) and the Hurwitz-Frobenius series (normed division algebras exist only in dimensions 1, 2, 4, 8). It records the coincidence that two independent constraints - the continuous floor and the discrete division-algebra ring - appear to converge on dimension four, while explicitly framing the connection of the model particle's vibrational degrees of freedom to four spacetime axes as the author's correspondence hypothesis (a working hypothesis, not a derivation). The state space is retained as the complex field; only the composition algebra of the four spacetime axes is linked to the quaternions. Bilingual edition (Japanese / English).
