Space, Matter and the Clock Do Not Share a Common Birth Condition — What a Seed Must Be to Make a Particle, and the Lower Bound of Resolution

空間・物質・時計の生成条件は一致しない——シードが粒子を生む条件と分解能の下限

Noriaki Kihara (WF System Co., Ltd.) · ORCID 0009-0004-6753-4020 · 2026-08-10 · v1 · Series: Generative Structure of Dimensions

Abstract

A controlled-experiment paper. The dynamics established in the previous work is imported read-only and left unmodified; only the seed placement, seed strength, seed phase, resolution and the number of divisions of the period are varied. Main findings: (i) space, matter and the clock do not share a common birth condition and become readable under different conditions; (ii) the onset of expansion is governed by the seed's complex sum (R^2=0.99987) while the time at which particle-like waves finish appearing is governed by the power placed on odd bands (tau ~ P^-1.073, R^2=0.996) - two different control quantities coexist in one dynamics; (iii) what looked like a threshold in seed strength is an artefact of the finite observation window, revealed by extending a run to 300000 updates; (iv) a seed on even bands only never fires the interaction at all; (v) six criteria are judged, and a strong seed produces matter and a clock at the cost of the determinacy of the third direction and of the condensate; (vi) the order of updates is not time - time becomes readable only after the state relations close into steady rotation; (vii) the divisor-class classification of the previous paper follows the number of divisions of the period (278 pairs, zero failures). 126 runs, 1174 MB of stored data, 462 per-run figures all visually inspected, program digests, pre-registered predictions and independent recomputation of every claimed number are included.
