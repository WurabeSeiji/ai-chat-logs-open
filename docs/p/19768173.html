Geometric Necessity of Spontaneous Symmetry Breaking: Three-Stage Derivation of the Higgs Mechanism

自発的対称性の破れの幾何学的必然性——ヒッグス機構の3段階導出

Noriaki Kihara (WF System Co., Ltd.) · ORCID 0009-0004-6753-4020 · 2026-04-26 · Series: Other / Not stored in this repository

Abstract

A structural answer to the Higgs mechanism problem posed in the companion paper on 6-dimensional hypercube particle classification (§9.11.7). Three stages — standing wave vacuum structure, symmetry breaking by scale separation, and mass generation by standing-wave coupling — are derived from geometric properties of the hypercube framework.
