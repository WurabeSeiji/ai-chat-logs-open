Interactions of Shape-Invariant Waves: Axis-Directional Displacement Transfer, Wave Packet Deformation, and Retroactive Construction of Causality (v6: axis-type symmetry reformulation)

形不変波の相互作用——軸方向変位転送・波束変形・因果の遡及的構成

Noriaki Kihara (WF System Co., Ltd.) · ORCID 0009-0004-6753-4020 · 2026-04-25 · v6 · Series: Three-Layer Projection Model of the Universe

Abstract

v6 reformulates the interaction axiom (I1) based on axis-type symmetry: the 6D axes are classified into position-type {x,y,z,t}, scale-type {R}, and color-type {Q}. A boson acts on ALL axes within each type where it has a non-zero component. This resolves the incompatibility caused by W8 v10 reassigning W± sign vectors from (0,0,0,±t,0,0) to (±x,0,0,0,+R,0). Key improvements: (1) CP violation correctly attributed to W± as the only boson that changes fermion t-axis sign, (2) W±/Z⁰ now κ_s=1 (localized carriers), enabling direct Higgs coupling, (3) 5-mode classification: spherical (κ_s=0: γ,g,G), localized carrier (κ_s=1: W±,Z⁰), standing (H⁰), direction-constrained (fermions), gravitational (G).
