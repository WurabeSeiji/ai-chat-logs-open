Abstract

The paper places nothing but a phase θ and an imaginary-direction amplitude A on a closed circle (S¹), together with the single condition that the sum of the true squares (no conjugation) of the components equals R², i.e. Σxₙ² + (iR)² = 0, the light-cone condition. Following the author's questions and corrections in their original order, it reads out quantisation (number of nodes), the fermionic/bosonic distinction (odd/even), localisation and the origin θ₀, the non-uniform geodesic length per gauge interval, the light cone R² = k² + m² as an identity (v² + (1/γ)² = 1 to 10⁻¹⁵), proper time, relativistic velocity addition, a Higgs-like pinned background as the cross term 2ab, and mass as the fraction of that pinned component. Extending the residents to the four-dimensional spherical shell S³ gives the necessity of three dimensions (associativity, Huygens' principle, curvature), spin (odd/even and SU(2)), charge (Hopf-fibre winding), observation as the cross term between particle and observer, and uncertainty and quantisation as consequences. The eleven stages that follow (mass, three dimensions, charge, the electron, α, a detour, ħ = 9α⁻¹ as an output in G = c = q₀ = 1, why 23/124, the four-dimensional Hurwitz lattice, spin, E₈ as a gluing of two Hurwitz quaternions) are written in the author's order, with numerical experiments E0–E5 re-run and seven interactive HTML figures included. α, 137 and 23/124 are one stage of the development, not the subject. The paper separates what has been verified, what is hypothesis, and the means of verification (the answer table of the Eleventh Thought Experiment).
