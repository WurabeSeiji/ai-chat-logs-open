Complex Rotating Wave's Area Cross-Term Curvature Oscillation Readout: Minimal Application to Gravitational Waves

複素旋回波の面積交差項による曲率振動の読出し

Noriaki Kihara (WF System Co., Ltd.) · ORCID 0009-0004-6753-4020 · 2026-09-01 · Series: 複素旋回波の面積交差項による曲率振動の読出し

Abstract

This research note proposes that the area cross-term (2iab) in complex rotating waves couples with curvature, enabling external observation as real-valued oscillations. We apply this to gravitational wave observations, particularly GW150914, using the Schwarzschild radius as the external readout scale. The natural frequency scale c/(2πr_s) ≈ 250 Hz matches the observed gravitational wave frequency band (35-250 Hz), suggesting potential connection to gravitational wave phenomena.
