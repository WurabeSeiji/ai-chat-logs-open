The Eleventh Thought Experiment: Emission Records of Two-Body Systems without a Background Spacetime - Computing Standard Theory (Dirac-Coulomb Bound States, 1PN Two-Body Gravity, Einstein A Coefficients) Referenced Only to the Rest Frame of an Absorber at Infinity, and Reading Out Emission-Absorption Relations for the Hydrogen Atom, Electron-Electron, Proton-Proton and the Hydrogen Molecule

第十一思考実験：背景時空を置かない二体系の放出記録

Noriaki Kihara (WF System Co., Ltd.) · ORCID 0009-0004-6753-4020 · 2026-10-07 · v1.0 · Series: Generative Structure of Dimensions

Abstract

A re-examination of the sixth thought experiment (10.5281/zenodo.22974631) and its supplement showed that gravity, the Coulomb force and the GW/EM radiation were updated there by externally supplied known equations, not by the distribution-array interaction itself. Attempts to derive the dynamics from the interaction (thought experiments 7-10, unpublished) and to re-implement the Einstein-Maxwell equations as discrete variational maps did not reach the goal. This paper is a separate line: it sets aside that derivation and builds a numerical program for the known standard theory - Dirac-Coulomb bound states with recoil and radiative-recoil corrections, 1PN two-body gravity with quadrupole gravitational waves, and Einstein A coefficients with a master equation - without any coordinate grid or metric in the state. Units are G = c = q0 = 1 (q0 = e/3); inputs are the fine-structure constant, the proton-electron mass ratio, the gravitational coupling ratio and the proton g-factor, all at known values. The only reference frame is the rest frame of a complete absorber at infinity, which plays the role of the asymptotic region of general relativity; the absorber is shown to be the family of cuts of each emission event's future null cone. Readouts use only the emission-absorption record (frequencies, rates, branching, times, photon pairs, directions, polarization). Four systems are run: the hydrogen atom cascade from 5p (13.054539268 eV emitted; agreement with measured levels and A coefficients to within the omitted QED and nuclear-size terms), electron-electron and proton-proton (no bound states; Mott cross section and quadrupole bremsstrahlung only), and H2 (301 rovibrational levels on the BO + adiabatic potential, E2/M1/GW cascades agreeing with Roueff et al. 2019 to a median 8e-4, radiative association H + H -> H2 + photon with k(T) = 2-4e-29 cm^3/s). The absorber is given as isotropic (T = 0 and 2.7255 K) and as anisotropic (dipole from 370 km/s, quadrupole 4e-6, N = 10^60 cells), and the three layers (temperature, direction/velocity/recoil, m-alignment) are read out from the record. The paper claims no new physics; it is the record of a reproducible computation and the reference (answer table) that a future distribution-array generator must reproduce. Japanese and English full texts, 16 figures, and all programs and data are included.
