Reading Out Yang–Mills and Dirac from the Product of Octonions ― A Thought Experiment in the Reverse Direction: The Hypothesis That Action, Distance, and Coupling Are One Number ―

八元数の積から、ヤン＝ミルズとディラックを読み出す

Noriaki Kihara (WF System Co., Ltd.) · ORCID 0009-0004-6753-4020 · 2026-11-20 · v1.0 · Series: Generative Structure of Dimensions

Abstract

The author has been working toward the goal of reading out spacetime, particles, and interactions from the relations among the states of a complex Hilbert space, without placing a background spacetime. In the preceding paper, the Lagrangian of quantum chromodynamics (QCD) was written down in octonions. While considering, in the course of that work, which operations on octonions could express the Yang–Mills equation and the Dirac equation, the author noticed that the direction was reversed. This paper records that thought experiment in the order in which it was carried out.Only one rule is placed: states are represented by octonions, and the product of octonions determines the next state. The product is computed not as the square of a norm but as the product as it stands, including the cross terms. Then a single number, the real part of the product of three octonions, plays three roles at once. (1) Action: read with all three as "links", it takes the form of the Yang–Mills action; read with the middle one as a "link" and the two ends as "matter", it takes the form of the Dirac action. (2) Distance: it is the distance, measured by triangulation, between the position pointed to by the product of two and the third. (3) Coupling coefficient: that distance becomes, as it stands, the weight of the action. No gauge field, no particle species, no kinds of interaction, and no arrangement of operations are given from outside. Only the initial state is given from outside.The forms in (1) and (2) follow from known facts and simple calculations, and are checked with the attached program (57 items). (3), and what lies beyond it, namely that "computing the coefficient of the action is, as it stands, reading out spacetime", is a hypothesis and requires verification. As the conclusion of the thought experiment, the author arrived at two hypotheses about the ruler that bears the distortion of gravity. Which is right, whether both are right, or whether both are wrong, is not known; deciding this is the next task. Readings noticed after the paper was written (such as reading the imaginary part of the triple product) are added at the end.This is a hypothesis paper and an interpretation paper. It contains no new theorem. Most of the parts are known. What this paper states is that, when the known parts are placed side by side, they can be interpreted in this way.
