Multi-Shell Large-Lattice Gravity, Causal Propagation, and Spin-2 Unlock of g_00(d) in the 3D Triangle-Loop Product (Part V)

三次元正方配列の三角形ループ積における多殻大格子重力、因果伝播、およびスピン 2 による g_00(d) 解禁（Part V）

Noriaki Kihara (WF System Co., Ltd.) · ORCID 0009-0004-6753-4020 · 2026-11-28 · v1.0 · Series: Generative Structure of Dimensions

Abstract

Part V following Parts I–IV (Concept DOIs 10.5281/zenodo.24622200, 24622565, 24625962, 24626592). Using exact zero-truncation closure dimensions (5D outer shell x 4D scalar / 6D spin-2 inner sector), we simulate nested multi-shell lattices O_8^{tensor K} tensor M_8 up to K=4 (N=32,768, 280 TB dense equivalent) deterministically without random numbers. We establish: (1) K=1 dense control agreement to 8.89e-14 in ||P||; (2) exponential critical horizon scaling epsilon_c(K) across K=1..3; (3) weak-field 1/5 per-shell decay (Delta phi(r) ~ 1/r under r=5^d) vs. strong-field exterior linear gradient Delta phi(d) ~ 3.8(K-d); (4) finite-speed causal propagation on K=3 (N=4,096); (5) universal weak-field g_00(d) lock (<=1.35e-13 rad) for scalar mass even on independent-clock H^{tensor 2}; and (6) 12-decade weak-field unlock of multi-shell g_00(d) by spin-2 (sigma_x) localized mass with mass-energy scaling Delta theta(0) ~ epsilon_sx^2 and shell-depth gradient.
