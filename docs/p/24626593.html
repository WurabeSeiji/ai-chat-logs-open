Part IV following Part I (10.5281/zenodo.24622200), Part II (10.5281/zenodo.24622565), and Part III (10.5281/zenodo.24625962). Demonstrates with deterministic machine-precision scripts (no random numbers): (1) exact 100%:0% self-dual/anti-self-dual chiral splitting of SU(2)_pure4 vs. SU(2)_space inside G2 (algebraic origin of maximal parity violation), (2) strict commutativity of all 12 Standard Model generators SU(3)_C x SU(2)_L x U(1)_Y with external SO(3) (all 36 pairs error 0.000e+00), (3) dynamical W1,W2,W3 mass generation via Higgs condensate P4_4 in Tri(T) vs. massless residual 8D symmetry, (4) bare Weinberg angle sin^2(theta_W) = 3/8 = 0.375000 from 64D trace ratios with zero free parameters and 4x4 electroweak mass spectrum [0, 0.25, 0.25, 0.40] (m_W^2/m_Z^2 = 5/8).
