Shape-Invariant Standing Waves on Closed Spheres: Stability per Dimension and the Geometric Necessity of 3+1 Spacetime

閉じた球面上の形不変定常波——次元ごとの安定性と3+1時空の幾何学的必然性

Noriaki Kihara (WF System Co., Ltd.) · ORCID 0009-0004-6753-4020 · 2026-04-24 · Series: Three-Layer Projection Model of the Universe

Abstract

We classify shape-invariant standing waves on the closed sphere S^n and prove that stable fundamental modes exist only for n=3, providing a geometric derivation of 3+1 spacetime dimensionality. The analysis uses Laplacian eigenvalues on S^n and hypercube-to-sphere projection to establish dimensional selectivity.
