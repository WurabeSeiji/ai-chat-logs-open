Geometric Symmetries of Central Projection: Mathematical Foundations of the Multi-Axis Model

中心投影の幾何学的対称性：多軸モデルの数学的基盤

Noriaki Kihara (WF System Co., Ltd.) · ORCID 0009-0004-6753-4020 · 2026-04-06 · Series: Central Projection Framework (Gnomonic Spacetime Geometry)

Abstract

In the preceding paper [1], central projection was used to map an n-dimensional tangent hyperplane onto the hypersphere S^n(R), and the induced metric tensor and Einstein tensor were derived. In this paper, we rigorously prove four geometric symmetries possessed by this central projection: (1) discrete stability — the mapping is globally regular and non-divergent regardless of whether coordinates take zero, positive integer, or negative integer values; (2) axis equivalence — the mathematical structure of the mapping is identical regardless of which axis of the background space is chosen as the projection center; (3) geodesic deviation — the curvature radius R generates deviation between geodesics, and the scalar quantity is measurable by an internal observer; (4) transformability of subjective coordinate systems — two subjective coordinate systems centered on different axes are mutually transformable via the composition of inverse projection and re-projection. These four properties are purely geometric propositions and contain no physical interpretation. Second paper in the series; see DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.19427780 for Paper 1.
