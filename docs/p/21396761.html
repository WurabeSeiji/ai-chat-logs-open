Selection of the State-Exchange Weight G_R = 1 - R and Candidate Correspondence with the Fine-Structure Constant: A Numerical Experiment

Release Notes: 交換重み G_R = 1 - R の選別と微細構造定数対応候補 v1

Noriaki Kihara (WF System Co., Ltd.) · ORCID 0009-0004-6753-4020 · 2026-07-16 · Series: Information Readout of Waves (Closed Two-Channel Systems)

Abstract

This preprint reports numerical experiments in closed phase systems showing that a state-exchange weight G_R = 1 - R can be sharply selected by exchange-scattering maps with harmonic degrees of freedom. The deepest candidate band corresponds, within the effective numerical precision of the experiment, to the low-energy fine-structure constant after the comparison G_R = sqrt(4 pi alpha). The work does not claim a derivation of alpha; it reports a candidate correspondence arising without inserting alpha into the scan.
