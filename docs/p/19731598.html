Shape-Invariant Traveling Waves on a 4-Dimensional Integer Lattice: Existence Conditions, Conservation Structure, and Self-Consistent Loops

4次元整数格子上の形不変移動波——存在条件・保存構造・自己整合ループ

Noriaki Kihara (WF System Co., Ltd.) · ORCID 0009-0004-6753-4020 · 2026-04-24 · Series: Three-Layer Projection Model of the Universe

Abstract

We formulate shape-invariant traveling waves on the integer lattice Z^4 and derive their existence conditions, conserved quantities, and self-consistent update loops. The framework provides the lattice-theoretic foundation for the mode classification in the companion paper on four modes of shape-invariant waves.
