An Exact Reduction of the Measure Problem: Finite Decomposition into Interference Granularity, Event Structure, and Conditioning (Dual Geometry of Wavelength and Frequency Spaces, Paper 16)

論文16:測度問題の厳密な縮約 — 干渉粒度・イベント構造・条件付けへの有限分解

Noriaki Kihara (WF System Co., Ltd.) · ORCID 0009-0004-6753-4020 · 2026-06-14 · v1.2 · Series: Dual Geometry of Wavelength and Frequency Space

Abstract

Paper 16 of the series Dual Geometry of Wavelength Space and Frequency Space. The system supplies path amplitudes with no free parameters; what is not fixed is the aggregation rule that bundles them into probabilities — what standard theory installs as the Born rule, by axiom. This paper does not select the rule; it gives an exact reduction of the selection problem. The historical proximity 0.0007 between the derived and counting measures is shown to be an artifact of two-outcome compression (a one-line lemma; the divergence jumps to 0.24 at s=11). Derived measures do not classicalize to counting measures (exhaustive at s=9/11/13), yet the two share their support exactly. Interference granularity is not reducible to convention: the mirror parent has W=0 exactly under channel-consistent aggregation while decaying perfectly normally at configuration granularity (W'=768=W'(A)); at m=4 the unit of cancellation refines to single channels; the occurrence condition is reduced to the four-value lock — algebra that is the object of proof. Conditioning requires the branch-channel stratum of the readout hierarchy (pairs indistinguishable by intensity fringes have different P(X)). Event structure is exactly one bit (one-shot vs sequential), completing a 2x2 table. Main theorem: the inventory-defined candidates organize without duplication into the product granularity x event bit x conditioning, each axis independent — with a decision table of experiments computable inside the model (two executed before publication). Under the reduction, the Born rule has the status of an effective law to be merged with in a suitable limit (convergence / suppression below statistical censorship ~1/sqrt(N) / prediction). Reviewed in two rounds under a two-party independent verification protocol. Japanese and English full texts. No physical identification is made.
