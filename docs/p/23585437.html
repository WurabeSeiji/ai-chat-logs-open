Did We Understand Complex Numbers Correctly? Where the Amplitude Lives, the Imaginary Reading, and Attribution across ℂ → ℍ → 𝕆

課題の整理（2026-10-23）

Noriaki Kihara (WF System Co., Ltd.) · ORCID 0009-0004-6753-4020 · 2026-10-23 · v1 · Series: Generative Structure of Dimensions

Abstract

The author's research programme reads out spacetime and particles from the relations alone of an anonymous set of complex numbers, and has used the structured number systems ℝ → ℂ → ℍ → 𝕆 in turn. This paper pauses midway and checks whether a symmetry already present at the stage of complex numbers has been overlooked; without the check, a property found with quaternions or octonions is misattributed as first appearing at that stage. One rule is set: a property may be said to belong to stage L only when it has been shown that it cannot be stated with the algebra of stage L−1 alone. The check starts from a naive question: when a state lies on a circle or a spherical shell, where does the amplitude live? (1) On a circle in the plane the amplitude |z| and the radius of curvature R are the same number; but R is invisible under geodesic translation and can be read only by a closed loop sweeping an area (holonomy), so the amplitude cannot be placed radially. (2) The amplitude lives in an unreadable direction orthogonal to both the circle and R; this is the origin of the Q axis of the spacetime equation x²+y²+z²+(it)²+(iR)²+(iQ)²=0 (three tangential directions = space, three normal directions t, R, Q unreadable, written with imaginary units). (3) Reading the amplitude as imaginary, with w = log u = φ + iθ, gives |cosh(w/2)|² = (cosh φ + cos θ)/2 and |sinh(w/2)|² = (cosh φ − cos θ)/2, whose sum is not 1 but cosh φ = γ; only on the section φ = 0 does cos² + sin² = 1 hold, which is the form of the Born rule. The normalization that makes it look like a probability is division by γ, and the dimension discarded is the rapidity. (4) The denominator |1+u|² = 2e^φ(cosh φ + cos θ) = 4e^φ|cosh(w/2)|² of the previous paper's coupling coefficient k = (1−u)/(1+u) = −tanh(w/2) is the same division; |k| = 1 holds at cos θ = 0 independently of φ, so the light cone is the self-dual point of coupling and distance. (5) Amplitude and phase are conjugate and dual, the duality being the 90° rotation of the exponent plane; number–phase conjugacy, time–frequency, Wick rotation and the Laplace variable are the same rotation. Which axis is read as imaginary and where one divides cannot be derived (self-reference) and is placed as an axiom; the Born rule and the invariance of the speed of light are two sections of this one axiom. (6) In complex numbers an imaginary amplitude is indistinguishable from the phase; quaternions give it a separate unit m ⊥ n, and the commutator [e^{ma}, e^{nθ}] = 2 sin a sin θ (m × n) generates a third direction, filling the three-dimensional imaginary part, i.e. space; the previous paper's k is the sign-reversed modified Rodrigues parameter. (7) In octonions, with two amplitude units (R and Q) and the phase unit, the associator first becomes non-zero and the R-type and Q-type amplitudes become algebraically distinct; with the spacetime-side quaternion a = t + xyz and the internal-side quaternion b = R + Q₁Q₂Q₃ the same 1+3 repeats, and Reading B is adopted: mass = spacetime curvature R (scalar part), charge = internal curvature Q (vector part), amplitude = |b|. (8) An attribution ledger lists what ℂ already has, what ℍ adds and what 𝕆 adds. All identities are checked numerically (11 items). The classification of claims, falsification conditions and next tasks are given. The Japanese version is the authoritative text; the English version is a translation.
