Relativity of Observation in Multiple Subjective Spaces: Geometric Consequences of the Symmetries of Central Projection

複数の主観空間における観測の相対性：中心投影の対称性の幾何学的帰結

Noriaki Kihara (WF System Co., Ltd.) · ORCID 0009-0004-6753-4020 · 2026-04-06 · Series: Central Projection Framework (Gnomonic Spacetime Geometry)

Abstract

This paper derives, as purely geometric consequences of Symmetry II (axis equivalence) and Symmetry IV (transformability of subjective coordinate systems) proved in Paper [2], the following four propositions: (1) From the ambient space R^{n+1}, n+1 subjective spaces can be simultaneously constructed by the choice of the projection center axis. Each subjective space possesses structurally identical geometric properties by Symmetry II. (2) An internal observer obtains only quantities constructed from the metric of their own subjective space and cannot access the value of the projection center axis, absolute position in the ambient space, or the existence of other subjective spaces. (3) What is the "projection center axis" for observer A appears as one of the subjective coordinates for observer B, and vice versa. The roles of axes that are equivalent in the ambient space are exchanged by the choice of the observer's standpoint. (4) There exists no geometric basis for designating a particular observer's standpoint as "correct." These are purely geometric propositions derived solely from the four symmetries of central projection established in Papers [1] and [2], and no physical interpretation is provided. Third paper in the series; see DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.19427780 for Paper 1, DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.19434932 for Paper 2.
