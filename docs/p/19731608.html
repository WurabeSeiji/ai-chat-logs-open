Mass Structure of Gauge Bosons: Higgs Non-Involvement Indicated by Sign Vectors

ゲージボソンの質量構造——符号ベクトルが示すヒッグス非関与性

Noriaki Kihara (WF System Co., Ltd.) · ORCID 0009-0004-6753-4020 · 2026-04-24 · Series: Other / Not stored in this repository

Abstract

Based on the sign vector table (Table A) of the companion paper on 6-dimensional hypercube particle classification and the interaction framework of shape-invariant waves, this paper shows that W± and Z0 boson masses arise from standing-wave coupling without requiring the standard Higgs mechanism. The sign vector structure directly determines which particles acquire mass.
