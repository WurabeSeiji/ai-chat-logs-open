Gnomonic Projection from $S^4$ and the Schwarzschild-like Form of the Induced Metric on a Four-Dimensional Subjective Chart (Paper 2)

$S^4$ からのグノモン投影と4次元主観座標図上の誘導計量の Schwarzschild 様の形（論文2：射影の数学的基盤）

Noriaki Kihara (WF System Co., Ltd.) · ORCID 0009-0004-6753-4020 · 2026-04-28 · v2 · Series: Black-Hole Thermodynamics Programme (incl. α-Identity)

Abstract

v2 (April 2026): Reference list refined per paper. External references corrected and finalized; no self-citations. v1 (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.19837590) remains the original publication.English: We study the gnomonic (central) projection from a four-dimensional sphere $S^4(R) \subset \mathbb{R}^5$ onto a four-dimensional tangent hyperplane, derive the induced metric, compute its Christoffel symbols, Riemann tensor, and Einstein tensor, and show that it satisfies the four-dimensional vacuum Einstein equations with a positive cosmological constant $\Lambda = 3/R^2$. Lorentzian continuation gives the de Sitter metric, and a Schwarzschild–de Sitter form arises with a mass parameter. Compared with the four-plus-one-dimensional Schwarzschild–Tangherlini metric.日本語: 4次元球 $S^4(R) \subset \mathbb{R}^5$ から4次元接超平面へのグノモン投影を扱う。誘導計量、Christoffel記号、Riemann/Einstein テンソルを計算し、$\Lambda = 3/R^2$ の4次元真空 Einstein 方程式を満たすことを示す。Lorentz連続化で de Sitter 計量、質量パラメータで Schwarzschild–de Sitter 形。4+1 Schwarzschild–Tangherlini 計量と比較。
