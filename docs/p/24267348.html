Emergence of Spacetime and Elementary Particles from a Bounded Hilbert Space and Octonions: From Photon Geometry and Gnomonic Central Projection to Open Problems in Background-Free Quaternionic and Octonionic Dynamics

有界なヒルベルト空間と八元数による時空・素粒子の創発

Noriaki Kihara (WF System Co., Ltd.) · ORCID 0009-0004-6753-4020 · 2026-11-16 · Series: Generative Structure of Dimensions

Abstract

A thought-experiment and dialogue record starting from the geometric property of photons (stationary in four-dimensional spacetime) and progressing through the gnomonic central-projection model, quaternion phase-gradient retention, and the non-associativity of octonions toward self-organization of particles and interactions from a bounded Hilbert space without assuming a background spacetime. Epistemic distinctions (identities/theorems, readings/interpretations, hypotheses, and open problems) and connections to prior published papers [S1]-[S15] are explicitly separated.
