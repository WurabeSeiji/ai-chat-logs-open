Abstract

Design specification (v2.1) of the particle data structure for the central projection framework. v2.1 revises v2.0 by removing the δδt field from both Particle and Mesh structures, because (1) the step(p) update δt+=δδt violates time-reversal reversibility (W3 information conservation), (2) single-particle δt change implicitly breaks energy/momentum conservation, and (3) GR free-particle principle requires acceleration to come from interactions. Also corrects Mesh.H_xyz_inphase to axis-specific modes (H_z = Higgs, etc.) per W11 v7. The Particle structure now has 4 fields (σ, V, δV, δt). All five operational principles (null transparency, in-place update, type integrity, INPUT/OUTPUT exchangeability, time-reversal reversibility) are rigorously verified.
