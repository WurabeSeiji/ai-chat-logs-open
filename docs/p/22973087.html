The Fifth Thought Experiment: Was There No Hidden State? - A Self-Audit of the Two-State Orbit Generation of the Fourth Thought Experiment, Tracking the State Closure from Five States to the Sixth Persistent State N = nu

第五思考実験：隠れた状態はなかったか

Noriaki Kihara (WF System Co., Ltd.) · ORCID 0009-0004-6753-4020 · 2026-09-26 · v2.1 · Series: Generative Structure of Dimensions

Abstract

In the fourth thought experiment (10.5281/zenodo.22919787), a state-dependent action S_n was applied sequentially to a two-component internal state X_n = (a_n, b_n), and a family of gravitational orbits including the periastron advance and the decay by the leading 2.5PN radiation reaction was reconstructed from the quadratic readout Phi(X_n) = (a_n^2 - b_n^2, 2 a_n b_n). When that implementation is audited as a flow of information, however, the processing phase chi, the semi-latus rectum p, the eccentricity e, the azimuth phi and the clock t are held and updated outside X_n, and X_n is a derived representation built from them. The reading that the orbit was generated by two states alone is therefore not exact. In the first stage of the audit, the five persistent logical states (chi, p, e, phi, t) that survive across macro steps were made explicit and re-encoded as Z_5 = (U, P, E, H, Q) = (e^{i chi}, p, e, e^{i phi}, e^{t/tau_0}). The RK4 sequential processing of Paper 4 could be rearranged into a synchronous state machine whose evaluation points, k_1..k_4, midpoints, composite increments and an 11-phase one-hot phase are explicit machine/work states. In long comparisons over 12 conditions, the difference from the reference update was at most 3.38e-16 in H and 4.00e-15 in Q. A further audit found that the symmetric mass ratio nu in the radiation-reaction kernel C_RR = (8/5) nu r^{-3} remained an external argument outside the state, so the system after the first audit was in fact Z_5' = F(Z_5; nu) and not fully state-closed. This paper internalizes it as the sixth persistent state, Z_6 = (U, P, E, H, Q, N) with N = nu, held in all 11 phases as the identity row N' = sum_j q_j (1 * N) = N inside the same synchronous map. In control experiments with the external nu argument removed from the new transition function, over 12 conditions x 4000 macrosteps the first five components of the old 5-state and new 6-state systems and the stored values of r, x, y, t were bitwise identical, with zero bitwise mismatches in N; over 3 representative conditions x 12000 macrosteps the maximum difference of the compared quantities was 0 and there were zero violations of the microstep identity of N. The error profile against the stored Paper 4 C1 data also coincided exactly with the old system. Raising the angular series to N = 12, 16, 20, 24 reduced the azimuth error after three orbits for the most severe case p = 24.3, e = 0.55 to 1.56e-7, 9.03e-10, 5.54e-12 and 3.55e-14; the time integration was likewise verified at orders 4, 6 and 8 with the same physical state. At least in these experiments, correcting the state closure and raising the numerical precision are separate operations. This paper does not claim that six states are mathematically minimal, that the 11-phase machine is necessary in nature, that the whole existing B scheme satisfies the strictest 'fully multiplicative internal operation' condition defined afterwards, or that general relativity is derived from this state system. What is shown is that a self-audit of the fourth thought experiment corrects the state closure from an apparent two states to five persistent states and then to six persistent states including the external nu, and that an orbit generation comparable with the known gravity model can be reconstructed as explicit states, synchronous updates and a non-feedback readout.
