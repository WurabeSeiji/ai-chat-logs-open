Construction Experiment of Multigauge Interference Readout Conserved Quantities in an ABC Closed Phase System

ABC閉鎖位相系における多ゲージ干渉読出し保存量の構成実験 v2

Noriaki Kihara (WF System Co., Ltd.) · ORCID 0009-0004-6753-4020 · 2026-07-13 · v2 · Series: Information Readout of Waves (Closed Two-Channel Systems)

Abstract

This release publishes a numerical constructive experiment on whether mass-like, momentum-like, and energy-like conserved readouts can be constructed from multigauge interference in an ABC closed phase system without assuming background coordinates. The paper reconstructs p_read, E_read, and R_read from multiple gauges, diagnoses the failure of simple q -> -q reversal under asymmetric R, and constructs an R-weighted generalized collision map conserving R*p and R*p^2. The release includes Japanese and English manuscripts, TeX/PDF builds, executed numerical scripts, result JSON/CSV files, figures, verification notes, and a reproducibility bundle.
