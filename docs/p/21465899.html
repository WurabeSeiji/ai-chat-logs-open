Linear Upper Bound on Generator Rank and Three-Direction Saturation of Spatial-Direction Readout in N-Body Complete Pairwise Relational Waves

N体完全二体関係波における生成子ランクの線形上界と空間方向読出しの三方向飽和

Noriaki Kihara (WF System Co., Ltd.) · ORCID 0009-0004-6753-4020 · 2026-07-21 · Series: Generative Structure of Dimensions

Abstract

Third paper of the Dimensional Generation Structure series. Extending the AB/ABC/ABCD complete pairwise relational-wave construction to general N bodies, this paper separates three quantities that grow at different orders: the number of internal relational waves M_N = N(N-1)/2 = O(N^2); the number of internal rotation modes r = rank(K)/2, bounded by the vertex decomposition theorem K = sum_k (c_k s_k^T - s_k c_k^T) so that rank K = 4, and the multi-dimensional kernel as a residual subspace with a unique projector but no unique internal basis. Japanese and English texts, TeX/PDF, four figures, experiment code, and reproduction data are included.
