Auditing the implementation of the fourth thought experiment by "which information is remembered until the next computation" revealed five states outside the apparent two, plus a mass ratio still passed in from outside
Intermediate values are made explicit states while they are needed, the 11-stage processing phase is put into the state, and the mass ratio is internalized as the sixth persistent state; the existing orbit output does not change
Numbers agreeing and the system being closed by the states alone are not the same thing: that is the fifth thought experiment
The sixth thought experiment goes in reverse: motion built from known theory, gravity + Coulomb with gravitational-wave and electromagnetic-dipole radiation, is placed as the answer, and I test whether it can be regenerated from the states alone
The generator holds no branch for particle A/B, gravity/electromagnetism or attraction/repulsion; only two relational quantities fixed by the charge condition are added, giving eight persistent states
During generation the answer orbit is never read: a single transition makes the next state from the previous state alone, all at once, and the read-out position and time are not fed back
For the reference case the final-time difference is about 1.5 x 10^-8, the accumulated-phase difference about 8.4 x 10^-11, and the drift of the three internal conserved relational quantities is 0
With the same update rule and only the charge condition changed, all five cases (8.35 to 3817 orbits) generated the orbital state to the end
In the slowest case the clock, stored as an exponential, exceeded the limit of finite precision; it is recorded as it is, without changing the rules to erase it
This is not a unification of gravity and electromagnetism, not a derivation of the Einstein-Maxwell equations, and not a proof that eight states are minimal: it is the stage of re-decomposing known physics into a form closed by states alone
