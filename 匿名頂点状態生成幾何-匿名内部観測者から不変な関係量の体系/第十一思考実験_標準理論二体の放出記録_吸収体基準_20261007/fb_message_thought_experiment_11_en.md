General relativity and electromagnetism could be computed without a background spacetime. For four two-body systems including the hydrogen atom, I made the answer key first

Details in the comments

My attempt to derive gravity and standard theory from the relations between waves and a universal interaction did not connect to the dynamics, and I wandered for two weeks; the attempt to convert the known equations into the universal interaction also came to a standstill
I changed course: keeping only the assumption of no background spacetime, I reformulated general relativity and Maxwell's electromagnetism; the state holds only levels, transition rates and the record of emission and absorption, with no coordinate grid and no metric
The systems are the hydrogen atom (proton + electron), electron + electron, proton + proton, and hydrogen atom + hydrogen atom; a neutron pair was set aside because it needs the strong force
It is an approximation with higher-order terms omitted, but apart from those it agrees with known values: the total emission of the hydrogen atom, 13.054539268 eV, equals the level drop, the ground level differs by 0.12 MHz, the radiation rates agree with NIST to within 0.012 %, and the hydrogen molecule agrees with a precision calculation of 4669 lines to a median of 0.08 %
In other words, today's general relativity and electromagnetism can be computed without first assuming a background spacetime; nothing new was discovered, and everything used is an existing equation or an experimental value
The hardest part was where the radiation goes: a proton and an electron alone have nothing to absorb it, so I placed a complete absorber (2.7255 K) sufficiently far away
This absorber is not a background spacetime but the cut of each emission event's future light cone at infinity, that is, the null surface of general relativity itself; it does not contradict the assumption of no background spacetime
If the absorber is perfectly isotropic, neither the velocity nor the orientation of the pair's centre of mass can be read; a slight anisotropy (an ellipse as seen from the pair) is needed, and with the cosmological value of 370 km/s the velocity was recovered from the records to 0.09 m/s
Two hydrogen atoms do not approach each other by gravity: gravity is 10^-36 of the electromagnetic force, and a pair bound by gravity alone would have a radius of 7 x 10^22 m (about 7 million light years)
I now have the answer table that must be reproduced once the dynamics can be derived from the universal interaction; the next question is whether this formulation can be replaced by a universal interaction made of arrays that distribute the relations between waves
