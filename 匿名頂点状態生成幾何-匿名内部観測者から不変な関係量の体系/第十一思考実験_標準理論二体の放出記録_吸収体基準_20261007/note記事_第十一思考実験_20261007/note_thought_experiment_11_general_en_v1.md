# General Relativity and Electromagnetism Could Be Computed without a Background Spacetime - Making the Answer Key First, for Four Two-Body Systems Including the Hydrogen Atom: the Eleventh Thought Experiment

At the end of the last article, I wrote this.

> Can the form of the interaction itself, given this time from the known theory, be derived from fewer relations? I return there.

I returned there.

And I wandered for about two weeks.

This article is about that wandering, and about the result I obtained after changing course.

## What I have been aiming at

From the beginning, my research has faced one direction.

Do not place a background spacetime.

That is, do not take the order of first preparing a grid of time and space coordinates and then putting particles and fields on top of it.

Instead, start from the relations between waves and from a universal interaction between waves.

From there, build a theory of gravity from which a curved spacetime can be read out, and a system that explains the behaviour of the particles of standard theory, as one and the same framework.

That was the goal.

## But it did not connect to the dynamics

Up to the fourth thought experiment, known gravitational orbits could be regenerated from states alone.

In the fifth and sixth thought experiments, gravity, the Coulomb force and two kinds of radiation could be put into one closed state update.

But the update rule for the radiation there was the known multipole formula, supplied from outside.

So in the seventh to tenth thought experiments I tried to derive the dynamics of gravity and the Coulomb force from the universal interaction itself.

None of them reached a two-body orbit with gravity included.

These are not published.

## Next, I tried to convert the known equations into the universal interaction

I changed my way of thinking.

Einstein's general relativity and Maxwell's electromagnetism are formulated in detail as equations.

Could those equations be converted into the universal interaction, which has the form of a variational equation?

In other words, could I translate from the side of the equations whose answers are known into this formalism?

This did not work either.

When run with an absorber attached, the system did not reach a lock and collapsed.

It came to a standstill.

## I changed course once more

Here I made the question one step smaller.

Replacing things by the universal interaction is set aside for the moment.

The only assumption kept is: no background spacetime.

Keeping only that assumption, can general relativity and Maxwell's electromagnetism be formulated again?

This looks easy.

It is only computing known equations with known numbers.

But they could not be used as they were.

In the usual textbook formulation, one first places the coordinates of spacetime, then places fields at each of its points (the electromagnetic field, the gravitational field, the so-called gauge fields), and solves the field equations.

That is, the existence of a background spacetime is assumed first.

For this purpose, that order cannot be used.

So I formulated it in a way different from the standard one.

What is held as the state is only the levels of the bound states of the two bodies, the rates of moving between the levels, and the record of emission to and absorption from outside.

No coordinate grid and no metric are held in the state.

There is exactly one reference frame.

It is the rest frame of the absorber at infinity, which appears later.

## I restricted it to four cases

I gave up the greed of formulating the behaviour of all particles.

Restricting further, I treated only four two-body systems.

The hydrogen atom, that is, a proton and an electron.

Electron and electron.

Proton and proton.

Hydrogen atom and hydrogen atom.

For these four I made the formulation and carried it through to numerical experiments.

Originally I had also considered neutron and neutron.

I wanted a pair of electrically neutral particles.

But to treat neutrons one has to introduce the strong force, and that cannot be formulated in the present framework.

So, as electrically neutral particles, I chose a pair of hydrogen atoms.

## Let me make it clear that this is an approximation

The present formulation is, after all, an approximation.

Higher-order terms are omitted.

For the hydrogen atom, the radiative corrections of quantum electrodynamics and the effect of the size of the proton are not included.

The distortion of spacetime by gravity is also put in only as a low-order correction, treating the neighbourhood of the two bodies as nearly flat.

For the hydrogen molecule I use the approximation that separates the motion of the nuclei from the motion of the electrons (the Born-Oppenheimer approximation and its first correction).

The computation is within that range.

## Even so, it agreed well

Apart from the omitted higher-order terms, the results agreed with known measurements and precision calculations to high accuracy.

Some examples.

- Hydrogen atom: I built 50 levels and computed the chain of radiation (the cascade) falling from an upper level downward. The total emitted energy, 13.054539268 eV, agrees exactly with the difference of the levels.
- Hydrogen atom: the ground-state energy differs from the measured value by 0.12 MHz. This is the part of quantum electrodynamics and of the nuclear size that was omitted.
- Hydrogen atom: the rates of radiation agree with the values of the standard database (NIST) to within 0.012 %. The width of the Lyman-alpha line is 99.71 MHz; the measured value is 99.7 MHz.
- Hydrogen molecule: I built 301 rovibrational levels and compared the rates of radiation with an existing precision calculation (Roueff et al. 2019, 4669 lines). The median relative difference was 0.08 %.
- Electron and electron, proton and proton: there are no bound levels. What remains in the record is only the scattering cross section and a tiny amount of bremsstrahlung.

In other words, it is this.

Today's general relativity and electromagnetism can be computed without first assuming a background spacetime.

Having found that out is, I think, the result of this work.

However, nothing new has been discovered.

Everything used is an existing equation or a number obtained from experiment.

## The hardest part was where the radiation goes

A hydrogen atom radiates electromagnetic waves.

Very slightly, it also radiates gravitational waves.

But a system of only a proton and an electron has nothing that absorbs that radiation.

If radiation goes out, it has to arrive somewhere.

Without a destination, neither the rates of radiation nor the records can be formulated.

So I assumed, sufficiently far away, a spherical shell that absorbs the radiation completely.

A dense, isotropic shell that completely absorbs both electromagnetic and gravitational waves.

To bring it close to the actual universe, its temperature was set to that of the cosmic background radiation, 2.7255 K.

## Does this not contradict the absence of a background spacetime?

This bothered me too.

Placing an absorber at infinity looks as if it contradicts the assumption of no background spacetime.

On examination, it did not.

In general relativity, light travels along the surface of the light cone.

This surface is called the null surface.

When radiation leaves a place at a time, the range it can reach is a cone opening toward the future.

The absorber at infinity turned out to be the family of the cuts where this future light cone meets infinity.

I had not placed an absorber; I had only been giving that name to the surface of the light cone that general relativity requires in the first place.

The right half of this figure is the whole of the present framework.

Every time there is an emission event, one record arrives at the absorber.

What is written in the record is: when, in which direction, at which frequency, with which polarization.

The motion of the pair is read out from these records alone.

Between one emission and the next, there is no readable change.

## When perfectly isotropic, no direction could be read

One more thing was found.

Suppose the absorber is perfectly isotropic and the pair is at rest inside it.

Then there is nothing that distinguishes one direction from another.

Neither where the centre of mass of the pair is moving, nor which way it faces, can be read from the record.

To read them, the absorber has to be slightly anisotropic.

When the pair moves relative to the absorber, the absorber looks slightly elliptical as seen from the pair.

That is the red dashed ellipse in the left half of the figure above.

It is nothing other than the geometry of the Doppler shift and aberration.

Here I put in the numbers observed in present-day cosmology.

The speed at which the solar system moves relative to the cosmic background radiation, 370 km per second.

Then, from the records of radiation from 200 hydrogen atoms, this speed could be recovered to 0.09 m/s.

From the 29 records of the 21 cm line of a single atom, it could be recovered together with the history of the kicks (the recoil) that the atom receives at each emission.

The temperature of the background radiation can also be read from the records.

In other words, with the numbers observed in the present universe, the readout of the motion of the pair is explained well.

## By the way, do two hydrogen atoms collide by gravity?

While computing the pair of hydrogen atoms, a naive question came up.

Do two hydrogen atoms attract each other by gravity and approach until they collide?

The answer is: they do not.

The gravity between the atoms is only about 10⁻³⁶ of the electromagnetic force.

If there were a pair of hydrogen atoms bound by gravity alone, its radius would be 7 × 10²² m, about 7 million light years.

That is comparable to the distance between galaxies.

In this computation, the gravity between the atoms was kept as a separate account for each level.

Its contribution to a level is 10⁻³¹ cm⁻¹, completely buried under the other effects.

On the other hand, the probability that two hydrogen atoms, on approaching, emit light and become a hydrogen molecule (radiative association) was 10⁻¹⁹ per passage.

This too falls within the range of existing estimates.

## What I am not saying

Let me state clearly, this time as well, what these results do not say.

No new law of physics has been found.

General relativity and electromagnetism have not been derived from the universal interaction either.

Nor have the field equations themselves been evolved in time. What was used is the levels of the bound states, the rates of radiation, and the records of emission and absorption.

Nor have the higher-order corrections been included. The radiative corrections of quantum electrodynamics, the size of the nucleus, and the higher-order gravitational corrections are omitted.

Nor do I claim where the absorber at infinity is in the actual universe. I wrote an interpretation that relates it to the cosmological horizon, but the limits of the approximation as a heat bath at a single temperature are written in the same place.

What was treated is the computation of four two-body systems under restricted conditions.

## What was found

What was found this time is this.

Without first assuming a background spacetime, the known general relativity and electromagnetism can be computed, referenced to the records of emission and absorption.

The complete absorber at infinity was not a background spacetime; it was a map onto the surface of the light cone (the null surface).

To read out the motion of the pair, the absorber has to be slightly anisotropic, and with the observed numbers of the present universe this is explained well.

And the biggest thing of all is that the answer key is now in hand.

The results of this computation are the answer table that must be reproduced when, in the future, the dynamics can be derived from the universal interaction.

The rates of radiation of the hydrogen atom, the 301 levels of the hydrogen molecule, the recovery of the velocity from the records.

If a universal interaction cannot reproduce these, it is wrong.

Until now, I had been searching for the form of the interaction while it remained vague what had to be reproduced.

That is why I wandered.

Can the present formulation be replaced by a universal interaction made of arrays that distribute the relations between waves?

That is the research question from here on.

The answer is at hand.

This time, it looks as though I can proceed without wandering.
