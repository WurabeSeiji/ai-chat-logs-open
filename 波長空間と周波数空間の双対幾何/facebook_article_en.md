Dual Geometry of Wavelength Space and Frequency Space — Reciprocal Duality and 4D Lattice Counting (observational paper now public)

See the comments for details.

Starting from the simplest relation between wavelength λ and frequency ν, namely λ = 1/ν (reciprocal duality), I imposed a "constant sum of squares" condition on each of the wavelength space and the frequency space, and observed — with elementary arguments only — what structure naturally arises.

Three things came into view. First, in one dimension the solution is fixed to essentially a single point, but with five components under one constraint, 5−1=4 leaves "four degrees of freedom." Second, taking logarithms with q=log λ, p=log ν turns the curved hyperbola into the straight sign-reversal line p=−q. Third, viewing the frequency (or wavelength) space as a 4-dimensional integer lattice and counting how many unit cells of side 1 fit fully inside a 4-dimensional ball of radius R gives, at radius 3, exactly 137 (by shells, 1+8+24+40+64) — a pure counting result.

That said, this work does not derive spacetime, mass, energy, or momentum, and does not identify ν with any of those physical quantities. It claims no new prediction or theorem; everything stays at the level of observing geometric and topological structure. Evaluation and interpretation are left to the reader.
