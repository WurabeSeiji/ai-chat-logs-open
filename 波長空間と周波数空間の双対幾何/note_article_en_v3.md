# "Position Had Never Been Read" — Dual Geometry Series, the Sequel: Papers 13–16 and a Survey, Published Together (from resolving the fifty-year question to the rigorous reduction of the measure problem)

A sequel to the observational series that began from νλ = 1. Four papers (13–16) and one survey — each bilingual Japanese/English, CC BY 4.0 — are now published on Zenodo. The previous installment (Papers 1–12) built up "what follows as theorems from three assumptions." This installment answers, with that inventory alone, the oldest question — **position, time, coordinates** — reduces the remaining **measure problem** to a decidable form, and finally, in a survey, accounts squarely for the first time for the **balance of connection to standard theory**.

Throughout, this series is an observational model. It claims no derivation of physical entities and identifies no model quantity with a physical quantity. All relations to standard theory are stated as structural correspondences; evaluation and interpretation are left to the reader.

## The inventory, restated — three assumptions only

- Assumption 1: reciprocal duality νλ = 1
- Assumption 2: the zero point ½ (the minimal conjugate width)
- Assumption 3: one asymmetric bit

To these are added two operating principles that fix how states are read (configuration reading: the state is the set of occupied cells; the record principle: records are appended from absence to presence). Continuous spacetime, amplitudes, wavefunctions, complex numbers, and the Born rule are — once again — not assumed.

## Paper 13: Position Had Never Been Read

"Can discrete states be mapped back and forth to position and time?" — the answer comes with **exactly zero additions** to the axiom inventory.

The heart of it: position is not a new degree of freedom but **the unread part of the dictionary**. Discrete position = the sign sector of the wave (quarter-period offsets: cos→sin→−cos→−sin). Only the way of reading the data already present in Paper 5's dictionary had to change. Space itself is derived: position space = the Pontryagin dual of the conserved-quantity lattice, and the three symmetries — translation, rotation, inversion — are theorems of duality, not assumptions.

The readout has three strata: intensity fringes alone resolve the relation classes into only 11; line positions into 13; and **only the branch channels (complex line coefficients) separate all 21 orbits completely** (11⊂13⊂21, exhaustive counts). The traditional fringe had been irrecoverably missing about half of the information — the title is the quantitative demonstration.

On the time side comes this installment's gem. That the squared clock frequency s ranges over **all odd integers** with spacing 2 is now a **proven theorem of unlimited range via Gauss's triangular-number theorem** (it reduces to (s−1)/2 being a sum of four triangular numbers). Meanwhile, t admits no independent inverse map: **t is not a fundamental degree of freedom — it merely appears to have been selected by observation.** That principle has now taken the form of a theorem.

## Paper 14: Time Direction and the Stage of Amplitudes

Then why does some particular axis look like "time"? Where does the complex i come from? The answer is two faces of one choice: **the choice of time direction = the choice of complex structure.** Exactly 16 complex structures are lattice-compatible, forming a single orbit of the symmetry group B₄ (16×24=384); the real axis can run along all four axes. The only readings shared by all markings are the norm (R) and the central character (Q) — which axis is time is gauge.

The transport phase is derived: the coefficient ratio between parent and daughter lines is fixed by trigonometric identities alone and **lands in Z₄ = {1, i, −1, −i} without exception** (exhaustive over three shells). The phase-assignment rule became unnecessary. And what standard theory installs as the symmetrization postulate (identical particles are not counted separately) comes out here as a **theorem of set structure** (the canonical-convention theorem).

## Paper 15: Projection onto xyztRQ

This paper closes the distance between "it should be possible" and "formulated and checked." The **explicit projection and inverse projection** from state data to spacetime coordinates (x,y,z,t) and ledger coordinates (R,Q) are constructed as a five-stage operation and machine-verified end to end: the composite round trip matches digits **500/500** on random points, and the map commutes with the conservation laws (charge-like parity conservation is a theorem of support arithmetic — 39.2% of raw cell combinations are violation candidates, yet violations among amplitude-carrying paths are zero).

Uniform motion is mapped as well. The absolute spectrum does not evolve; only the phase does — **velocity exists nowhere in a single record; it exists only in the sequence of records.**

## Paper 16: The Rigorous Reduction of the Measure Problem

The largest remaining blank is the measure — the rule of probability. The system supplies path amplitudes with no free parameters: the raw material is complete, but how to bundle it (the aggregation rule) is undecided. Standard theory settles this with an axiom: the Born rule. This paper **selects no aggregation rule.** It rigorously reduces the selection problem itself.

A sample of the findings: the historical proximity once read as "close to a measure candidate" (0.98195 vs 0.98263) turned out to be an artifact of compression into a two-outcome space — at the next shell, where three outcomes open up, the divergence **jumps by three orders of magnitude**. Moreover, "which alternatives are added coherently" (interference granularity) is not a convention: the mirror parent **vanishes exactly (W=0)** under channel-coherent aggregation while remaining **perfectly healthy (W′=768)** at configuration granularity — same amplitudes, same states; only the bundling decides life or death, exactly. The condition for this cancellation has been pinned to a provable algebra called the "four-value lock."

Conclusion: the remaining freedom of aggregation reduces to a finite decision structure — **interference granularity × one event-structure bit × conditioning** — and the adjudication between candidates stands as a **table of experiments computable inside the model**. The Born rule is placed in the status of "an effective law to be merged with in the appropriate limit," with the triple acceptance criterion of convergence, suppression, and prediction.

## The Survey: From One Relation, One Constant, and One Bit

Until now the series deliberately refrained from speaking of the connection to standard theory, to avoid misreading and dismissal. With all sixteen papers published, the survey accounts for the balance for the first time.

Three main results. (1) The kinematic correspondence dictionary is filled — every entry machine-verified, every source published. (2) **Five items that standard theory installs as axioms — symmetrization, time, measurement, dimension, charge conservation — this system holds as theorems.** (3) Only two entries remain blank: the measure and the action.

And the survey's central thesis: a completely filled dictionary would be a mere re-notation, carrying no empirical content. **The points where the connection is incomplete are the only places where the two theories make different claims and observation can adjudicate** — the remainder is at once a deficiency and a map of decidability. The survey dissects these points one by one and identifies their structural causes.

A postscript answers the anticipated criticism, "isn't this just complex numbers in disguise?" The core of the reply: state that criticism rigorously, and it becomes precisely the isomorphism theorem this series intends to prove next. **The criticism is not an enemy but a roadmap.**
