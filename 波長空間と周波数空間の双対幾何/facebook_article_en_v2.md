The observational series that started from the simplest relation between wavelength λ and frequency ν — the reciprocal duality νλ = 1 — is now complete with twelve papers. The eight new papers (Papers 5–12) have just been released on Zenodo (bilingual JA/EN, CC BY 4.0, with all verification scripts and 41 research notes public).

Only three assumptions are used: the reciprocal duality νλ = 1, the zero point 1/2 as a minimal width, and one asymmetric bit — the conservation law rides only one side of the duality. Continuous spacetime, amplitudes, wave functions, and collapse are not assumed. The question is single: from this inventory alone, how much of what looks quantum and spacetime-like follows as theorems?

What came out: indistinguishability and the exclusion principle become theorems rather than assumptions; the stable species narrow down to exactly {1, 3, 5}, and that value is reached by two independent computations — counting and wave interference. The stability of composites is no accident but a quantization of the deformation space; large clumps cannot exist as single entities, so hierarchization is forced. Without assuming any gauge principle, a connection and the continuous group SO(4) emerge; the dimension d = 4 is pincered by three independent mechanisms; measurement becomes the appending of a record (absence → presence); and the instantaneous correlation of entanglement becomes a statement about layers, not about speed.

Throughout, this series is an observational model. It does not derive spacetime, mass, or energy, and it does not identify model quantities with physical ones (the 137 that appears in the counting is not identified with the fine-structure constant). Every relation to standard theory is stated as a structural correspondence; evaluation and interpretation are left to the reader.
