Did we understand complex numbers correctly? Ask where the amplitude lives, and the Born rule and the invariance of the speed of light become two sections of one axiom

Before going on to quaternions and octonions, I went back to complex numbers to check whether I had overlooked a symmetry already present there and credited it to quaternions or octonions
One rule: a property may be said to belong to stage L only when it has been shown that it cannot be stated with the algebra one stage down
The amplitude cannot live on the radius (the radius of curvature). Curvature can be read only by the area of a closed loop, so an amplitude placed radially would turn every change of amplitude into a change of curvature
The amplitude lives in an unreadable direction orthogonal both to the circle and to the radius. This is the origin of the Q axis in the spacetime equation x² + y² + z² + (it)² + (iR)² + (iQ)² = 0
Read that amplitude as imaginary, and one and the same complex exponential gives cos² (the form of the Born rule) on the section φ = 0 and cosh φ = γ (the Lorentz factor) on the section θ = 0
The normalization that makes it look like a probability is division by γ, and the one dimension thrown away there is the rapidity. The Born rule and the invariance of the speed of light are two sections of one axiom
The denominator of the previous paper, |1 + u|² = 2e^φ(cosh φ + cos θ), is the same division as the Born-rule normalization, and the light cone |k| = 1 is the self-dual point of coupling and distance
In complex numbers an amplitude read as imaginary cannot be told from the phase. Quaternions tell them apart with a separate unit, and their non-commutativity generates the third direction, that is, space
In octonions the associator first stands with the triple of two amplitude units (R and Q) and the phase, and mass-type and charge-type amplitudes become algebraically distinct. Mass = spacetime curvature R, charge = internal curvature Q
Sorted into an attribution ledger, what quaternions and octonions add is only space and the distinction between mass and charge; the rest was already in the complex numbers. All 11 identities are checked numerically
