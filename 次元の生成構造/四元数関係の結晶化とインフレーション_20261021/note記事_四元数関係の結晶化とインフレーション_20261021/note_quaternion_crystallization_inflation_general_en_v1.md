# Placing Nothing, Spacetime Is Born, the Universe Inflates, and Mass and Charge Appear - A "Crystallization" Hypothesis Built with Quaternions and Octonions

Using quaternions and octonions, I have built a hypothesis that can explain, from nothing but a set of states expressed by complex numbers, how spacetime is born, how inflation happens, and how mass and charge arise and particles appear.

Until now I have been studying, without assuming a background spacetime and using only the relations between states, the mechanism by which general relativity and the Standard Model hold with high accuracy in the locally flat approximation. In the course of this work, a hypothesis with some real promise has emerged.

Read as it stands, this probably sounds like crackpot theory.

But the content has been worked on seriously. The paper is published on Zenodo with a DOI, and anyone can reproduce it, including the programs that make the figures. The paper also states the falsification conditions: what would have to come out right for it to be correct, and what would show it to be wrong.

In this article I introduce what is inside, with figures.

## The starting question

General relativity and the Standard Model cannot be unified well.

Yet when we approximate them as locally flat, both hold with extremely high accuracy.

Why?

Is there a way to explain this without bringing in many arbitrary parameters?

This is the starting point of this research.

The answer was crystallization, in the sense of condensed-matter physics.

## 1. Distance can be read from the relation between two states

First, how do we read distance where there is no space and no ruler?

Two complex numbers of the same size form an isosceles triangle together with the origin.

Just as astronomers measure distance from an apparent angle, distance can be read from how wide this triangle opens.

[Insert Figure 2: fig02_angular_distance_coupling_en.png]

Figure caption: The isosceles triangle formed by two complex numbers of the same size (left), and the coupling strength g and the distance D (right).

If the phases are aligned, the two appear superposed and are infinitely far apart. They do not act on each other.

If the phases are opposite, the two cancel and are in contact. The action is at its maximum.

If distance is read as the weakness of the coupling of action, this behaviour is natural. It has the same form as the reflection coefficient of an electrical circuit.

When this is extended to quaternions (pairs of complex numbers), the coupling strength becomes a three-dimensional vector.

In other words, from the relation between two states alone, a three-dimensional point with a direction and a distance can be read.

[Insert Figure 3: fig04_quaternion_S3_to_R3_en.png]

Figure caption: Extension to quaternions. Projecting the relative phase on the three-dimensional sphere S³ from the point opposite the observer gives a three-dimensional vector.

Moreover, this vector is exactly the central projection found in the previous paper, and a relation with coupling strength exactly 1 corresponds to a relation on the light cone.

## 2. Local flatness is not an approximation but a property of the readout

This readout preserves angles.

For that reason, an observer who looks around with itself at the centre cannot distinguish the following.

Whether space is uniformly curved or flat.

The overall size (scale).

Which map it is looking through.

All that is visible is the component by which shapes are distorted depending on direction (tides).

This is the same structure as the equivalence principle of general relativity: a uniform gravitational field can be removed locally, but tides cannot.

Here lies one answer to the starting question.

**Local flatness is not an approximation but a property of a readout centred on the observer.**

## 3. Reading connections from points without names

Then how do we decide which state is connected to which, without supplying it from outside?

Quaternions of size 1 are points on a three-dimensional sphere inside four-dimensional space.

Consider the smallest convex shape (the convex hull) that wraps a collection of points without names. Its faces are tetrahedra, and we obtain a truss (a framework) filling the sphere with tetrahedra.

The convex hull does not depend at all on the order or the numbering of the points. The connections are decided without attaching names.

[Insert Figure 4: fig05_convex_hull_delaunay_en.png]

Figure caption: The convex hull of a set of points without names (left), and the Delaunay triangulation seen through the projection centred on the observer (right). In an example one dimension lower, I confirmed numerically that the faces of the convex hull of 30 points all coincide with the 53 triangles of the Delaunay triangulation of the projected points.

## 4. Not being uniquely determined gives birth to time

Here a difficulty appears.

When five or more points lie on the same sphere, the way of dividing into tetrahedra is not determined uniquely.

Normally this is a defect to be removed.

But I placed it as a principle.

Since the structure is not determined uniquely, building the structure is not solving equations to get one answer; it can only be defined as a process with a procedure and a history.

The sequence of that process becomes time, and the history of where and in what order things were decided can become the origin of the arrow of time.

Applying the way of counting rigidity used in structural engineering, three states come into view.

With no framework at all, no structure forms.

With everything connected, it becomes a rigid crystal and cannot move.

Only the partially connected state in between combines a framework with room for rearrangement.

[Insert Figure 5: fig06_three_phases_rigidity_en.png]

Figure caption: The three phases of projection. From the left: zero projection (no structure forms), partial projection (a framework and room for rearrangement coexist), full projection (a rigid crystal that does not evolve).

The room for rearrangement corresponds to what quantum theory calls indeterminacy.

In other words, the side of relativity (local flatness) and the side of quantum theory (indeterminacy and process) come out as two faces of one and the same readout.

## 5. Regular tetrahedra cannot fill flat space

Placing five regular tetrahedra around one edge leaves a gap of 7.36°. Regular tetrahedra alone cannot fill flat three-dimensional space without gaps.

On the curved three-dimensional sphere, however, there is a regular polytope that 600 regular tetrahedra fill exactly (the 600-cell). Its 120 vertices are quaternions of size 1.

[Insert Figure 6: fig07_tetrahedral_frustration_600cell_en.png]

Figure caption: View along a shared edge. In flat space a gap remains (left); on the three-dimensional sphere it closes (right).

The space of states itself is the three-dimensional sphere. But the observer's readout is locally flat.

So the part that cannot close necessarily remains as lines of defects. The defect lines carry a deficit of angle, and that carries curvature (gravity).

Without any parameter given from outside, geometry enforces partial projection. This is one of the key points of this hypothesis.

## 6. Inflation without an inflaton field

From here comes the biggest claim of this article.

The theory that there was a rapid expansion (inflation) at the beginning of the universe was proposed by Guth in 1981 and is now part of standard cosmology.

However, to cause the expansion, this theory places a field called the inflaton and its shape (the potential). The nature and origin of this field are unknown. Its shape is tuned to fit observations.

In this hypothesis, the inflaton field is not needed.

Supercooled water freezes all at once when a small seed enters. In the same way, when a seed enters a highly symmetric state, tetrahedra fix one after another.

When one tetrahedron attaches, one face closes and three new faces appear. So the number of faces that can bond grows by up to three times per round.

[Insert Figure 7: fig08_geometric_growth_efold_en.png]

Figure caption: The tree of faces (left), and how the number of blocks grows for each factor r (right). The dashed line is an illustrative model including the effect of closing, and is not derived.

With a factor of 3, the 60 e-folds (about 10²⁶ times) needed by standard inflation are reached in about 164 rounds.

Three points matter.

What is expanding is not space. What increases is the number of fixed relations (blocks). For an observer who cannot see the overall size, this is read out as exponential expansion.

The factor is decided by geometry, namely the number of faces of a tetrahedron. There is no knob to tune.

As faces close, the factor approaches 1 and the expansion stops. Guth's original theory had the problem that the expansion could not end, but here that problem does not arise, by structure.

Further, the latent heat released by crystallization corresponds to reheating, and the frozen defect lines correspond to the primordial fluctuations.

The inflaton was not replaced by something else. The need to place anything that causes the expansion has disappeared.

There is other research aiming at inflation without an inflaton. But as far as I have looked, each of them places something in place of the inflaton (higher-order curvature terms, an interaction potential, a network and energy). I did not find a construction that places no field, no potential and no background space, and decides both the expansion and its end by counting faces alone.

## 7. The same crystallization also explains the Higgs mechanism and the origin of mass

From here on is what I examined after publishing the paper.

Crystallization, that is, a seed entering a highly symmetric state and the structure becoming fixed, is exactly what physics calls spontaneous symmetry breaking.

The Higgs mechanism, which gives mass to elementary particles, is also spontaneous symmetry breaking. In the Standard Model, inflation (the inflaton field) and symmetry breaking (the Higgs field) are events caused by separate fields at separate times.

In this hypothesis, both become one process of crystallization.

The shape of the Higgs field (the Mexican-hat potential), like the inflaton potential, is a part whose shape was fixed by hand. In this hypothesis, both become unnecessary for the same reason.

On mass, the paper places the following hypothesis.

**Mass is the square of the amplitude of a standing wave, and is read out by the observer as a ratio of amplitudes.**

It is the same idea as a box enclosing light having mass as a whole. There is no need to place coupling constants from outside.

## 8. Charge, and the ratio of charge to mass

On charge, earlier research had found that the elementary charge can be read as a transmission rate. The part that is not reflected is the charge.

The coupling strength in this paper, as written above, has the same form as the reflection coefficient of an electrical circuit.

Further, computing the coupling of two quaternions of different sizes, it divides into a scalar part and a vector part.

The scalar part is the mismatch of amplitudes and corresponds to mass.

The vector part is the coupling of phases with a direction and corresponds to charge.

Mass and charge appear at the same time, as two components of the readout of one relation. The ratio of charge to mass is not a ratio of two quantities fixed separately, but the ratio of two components of one and the same quantity.

## 9. The spacetime equation was the null condition of octonions

I have long used the equation that combines spacetime with the internal space,

x² + y² + z² + t² = R² + Q₁² + Q₂² + Q₃².

This time it turned out that this is exactly the condition that the norm of a number called a split octonion vanishes.

Moreover, it is the same as the condition that the quaternion on the spacetime side and the quaternion on the internal side have equal size.

## 10. As a framework for a grand unified theory

Let me put this together with my earlier research.

The symmetries of the Standard Model (the 16 particles of one generation and their charges) have been derived in earlier research, apart from the coupling constants.

Mass and charge come out as two components of the readout of the coupling in this paper.

Inflation and symmetry breaking are one process of crystallization.

The side of general relativity (local flatness, the equivalence principle, curvature as defects) and the side of quantum theory (indeterminacy, process, history) are two faces of the same readout.

However, all the readouts so far presupposed that there was a structure (a lattice, connections) to read out from. Where that structure comes from was the last wall.

**This time, a mechanism by which the crystal structure of spacetime forms spontaneously has been found, and that wall has been crossed.**

Unlike an ordinary grand unified theory, which unifies the forces after placing a background spacetime, this is a framework that brings out everything from one mechanism, including the origin of spacetime itself.

## 11. What remains, and the honest limits

What remains is the following.

The connection to the large symmetry called E₈.

The coupling constants of the strong and weak forces.

The numbers for the ratios of particle masses.

Comparison with the quantities measured in the cosmic microwave background (the tilt of the fluctuation spectrum and the strength of gravitational waves).

And verification.

Here there is an honest limit.

To follow the crystallization of the whole universe directly by numerical experiment, one would need to track more than 10⁶⁰ blocks over some hundred and thirty or more rounds. This does not run directly on any computer, not only for an independent researcher.

What I can do is check the local rules at small scale, extrapolate analytically, and leave predicted numbers that can be compared with observation. Verification at full scale will be the work of research groups with large computing resources.

The falsification conditions for that are written in the paper. For example, if no stage appears in which the factor clearly exceeds 1 as the structure fixes from a seed, I will drop the claim about inflation.

Place nothing from outside. No names, no connections, no fields, no potentials.

From there, not being determined uniquely gives birth to time, being unable to fill flat space gives birth to curvature, crystallization gives birth to the expansion of the universe and to symmetry breaking, and the readout of one relation gives birth to mass and charge.

Not explaining by adding, but being forced by what is taken away.
