QCD Expressed in Octonions: Writing a One-Line Formula from a Public Lecture in Octonions

Details in the comments

After attending Professor Tetsuya Onogi's lecture in "Science Night 2026," a public lecture series of The University of Osaka, I published a paper that writes the QCD Lagrangian in octonions as it stands
When one imaginary unit of the octonions is fixed, the eight numbers split into one complex number and three complex numbers, and the latter correspond to the three color components of a quark
The automorphisms of the octonions that do not move that unit form SU(3), so the eight of the gluons appears inside the octonions (the known construction of Günaydin and Gürsey)
The gluons are not the eight components of the octonions themselves; they are on the side of the transformations acting on the octonions
The Lagrangian can be written in the language of octonions without changing its form, and its content is the same as standard QCD
Expanding the gluon-only term in components gives seven conditions determined by the multiplication table, and 15 − 7 = 8 agrees with the number of gluons
The Wilson action on the lattice can also be written as a trace over the eight components of the octonions
Only the part concerning color appeared from the octonion side; spacetime, spinors, masses, and the coupling strength are still placed from outside
The paper contains no new theorem and does not derive QCD from the octonions
It serves as a reference for checking answers on the strong force in future work on reading out interactions without a background spacetime
