# Only Time Could Not Be Read — The Universe Was Building Its Own Rulers and Clocks

In the previous article, I reported that our wave model ran in a single pass, with no switches, from a vacuum through inflation, condensation into three directions, precipitation of matter, and finally that matter moving without changing its shape.

But there was one thing I had to confess.

The rulers and the clocks were being supplied by us, from the outside.

To watch matter "move," we had brought in a grid for measuring position and a clock for keeping time, by hand, as laboratory equipment. That does not qualify as "space and time were born." The human who inserted the instruments simply brought space and time along with them.

The question of this paper is:

Can the universe build its own rulers and clocks, by itself?

The answer was yes, it can. And as the investigation went on, one strange thing remained at the very end — a quantity that cannot be read, no matter what. Exactly one.

## The true identity of the "three directions" was not three axes

The endpoint of inflation is a structure in which the waves have condensed into three directions. We had been calling this "three axes," but when we measured its contents precisely, that turned out to be wrong.

The truly independent entity is a single rotating plane — just one. The third direction is not an independent axis; it is a dependent that comes along automatically as the rotation axis of that plane (the normal vector computed from the plane's two directions agrees with the dynamical rotation axis to 99.65% as N is increased).

In other words, three-dimensional space is not assembled out of three rods.

A single property — "being able to rotate" — becomes three directions when it is read out in real numbers.

Two directions' worth from the rotation plane, one direction's worth from the rotation axis. Two plus one. Here is a concrete candidate mechanism for the question "why is space three-dimensional?"

## The ruler — the x and y axes are built by demodulating with the clock

So how do we read an x axis and a y axis out of that plane?

Think of a radio. To extract music from a radio wave, you mix it with a reference signal inside the receiver and "demodulate" it. The condensate is doing the same thing — to itself.

The condensate rotates as a whole with a single phase — this is the reference signal (the clock). When the complex coordinate of the plane is demodulated against this clock, two components can be extracted: the in-phase component and the quadrature component. Those are x and y. And z is the direction of the rotation axis.

Here is the important part. x and y are orthogonal not because there are two orthogonal rods on the side of the physical substance. It is because the real part and the imaginary part of a complex number are orthogonal — that is, the orthogonality is manufactured by the readout mechanism. In the language of physics this is homodyne detection itself, but unlike in optics, no experimenter needs to bring in the reference signal. The universe supplies its own reference signal to itself.

## The clock — proper time is a collective order enforced by entrainment

There was a surprise on the clock side as well.

All the planes occupied by the condensate rotate monolithically at a single frequency (measured at high resolution, the spread is within 1%. The "frequency ladder" that had been visible at low resolution was a fake created by the resolution of the measuring instrument — we report this honestly in the falsification-and-correction section of the paper).

And this clock has enforcement power.

![Figure 1](fig_s3_clock_entrainment_v1.png)

Figure 1. Right panel: when a perturbation that carries no clock of its own is added to the condensate (red line), it cannot keep its own rhythm and is forced into synchronization with the condensate's clock. This is the same phenomenon as pendulum clocks synchronizing through a shared wall — entrainment.

Every wave that has no clock of its own is entrained by the condensate's clock and brought to rest. Rest, it turns out, does not mean "zero velocity" — it means complete synchronization with the background clock.

Turning that around, we can say: the only things that can move are those that can keep beating their own rhythm against the pull of entrainment. And in this series, the things that "carry their own clock" already have a name — mass.

Mass → own clock → time → motion. This chain has now been connected by experiment.

## In the four-dimensional readout, matter moves in uniform straight-line motion

With ruler and clock both in hand, we re-read the motion of the matter packet from the previous paper, in full four dimensions: xyz plus proper time τ.

![Figure 2](fig_s8_translation4d_v1.png)

Figure 2. Top left: a sequence of equally spaced points travels straight through three-dimensional space. The remaining three panels: the projections onto the x–τ, y–τ, and z–τ planes are all straight lines. From τ = 0 to 900, all ten points show deviation 0.000. The shape does not degrade at all.

Textbook uniform straight-line motion holds — not on coordinates handed in from outside, but on coordinates the universe built for itself.

## Mass is "disorderliness," and the sea is heavy

Mass can be read out too. Measure how much the waves of the two readout channels fail to be combined into a single wave within the window — the non-coherence (disorderliness) — and that quantity behaves as mass. A perfectly aligned wave (a laser-like state) has zero mass = light-like. A wave that cannot be unified carries mass.

Measuring the distribution revealed something interesting. The modes of the thermalized sea are almost all pinned at maximal disorderliness — the sea is heavy. Light (light-like) states do not exist inside the sea; only well-ordered, coherent structures are light. The more disordered, the heavier; the more ordered, the lighter — the opposite of intuition, and a clean structure.

Charge can also be read as a winding number, but here I will write honestly: the charge scatters across multiple quantized values and refuses to settle at ±1. Why the elementary charge is a single value is stated explicitly as a major open problem of this series.

## And exactly one unreadable thing remained

Let us count up everything that can be read. Three spatial axes. Proper time. Mass. Charge.

What cannot be read is — coordinate time. That one thing alone.

![Figure 3](fig_s7_cone_readout_v1.png)

Figure 3. What can be read, and what cannot. Green: readable quantities. Red — coordinate time alone cannot be read directly by any measuring device.

Coordinate time means "a single time axis shared everywhere in the universe." No matter where we searched in our experimental system, we could not build a measuring device that reads it directly. Coordinate time shows its face through only two routes: as the remainder left after subtracting mass and charge from the total, or when the clocks at many locations are cross-checked and a choice is made — "let us agree that there is a time we all share."

Why is time the only thing that cannot be read? The candidate answer is this. Mass and charge are structures of the wave — they appear in an instantaneous snapshot. But time is the flow itself. It does not appear in a snapshot. From inside the flow, the flow cannot be read.

And this result meshes precisely with a deep structure of general relativity. In relativity, "proper time (what a clock measures) is physics, while coordinate time is merely a choice of coordinates — a convention." But there, this is placed at the beginning as a principle. In our model, in the opposite direction, this distinction was derived from the structure of the readout.

## Summary

- We stopped supplying rulers and clocks from outside, and re-derived them from the condensate itself

- The true identity of three dimensions is 2 + 1: one rotating plane plus its dependent rotation axis. Three-dimensionality is the real-number readout of "being able to rotate"

- x and y are the orthogonal components the universe obtains by demodulating itself with its own clock. Orthogonality is manufactured by the readout

- Proper time is a single collective clock, enforced on the surroundings by entrainment. Rest = synchronization to the clock; motion = the privilege of carrying one's own clock (mass)

- Matter moves in uniform straight-line motion on the four-dimensional coordinates the universe built for itself (zero deviation at every point)

- Mass is disorderliness. The sea is heavy. The ±1 problem of charge is explicitly left open

- The only unreadable quantity is coordinate time. Proper time is physics; coordinate time is a choice — and this very distinction came out of the model

Spacetime may not be a container in which things are placed. A system of readouts that the condensed waves built up in order to observe themselves — that is the picture this paper is pointing at.
