background-free \textbf{triangle-loop product}
\(\mathrm{Tri}(T)_{ijk} = \sum_{p,q,r} T_{ipq} T_{qjr} T_{rpk}\) on

whether \textbf{spin (spatial rotation representations), exact gauge
