\subsection{Abstract}\label{abstract}

We investigate the origin of discrete and exponential mass/energy
hierarchies in the background-free real 3-tensor triangle-loop product
\(\mathrm{Tri}(T)_{ijk} = \sum_{p,q,r} T_{ipq} T_{qjr} T_{rpk}\) on the
Layer-1 time crystal \(H = O_8 \otimes M_8\) (\(N = 64\)). Placing
localized probes corresponding to all four Hurwitz subalgebras of the
outer octonion algebra---\(\mathbb{O}_8\) (\(d=8, m=0\)),
\(\mathbb{H}_4\) (\(d=4, m=1\)), \(\mathbb{C}_2\) (\(d=2, m=2\)), and
\(\mathbb{R}_1\) (\(d=1, m=3\)), where \(m = \log_2(8/d)\) is the
codimension---under identical conditions
(\(\epsilon = 0.05, 0.10,\; \tau = 30\), zero randomness), we establish
four exact algebraic laws:

the resulting single-layer rational laws with the exact tensor-product
homomorphism of \(\mathrm{Tri}\).
