\subsection{Abstract}\label{abstract}

Parts I {[}P1{]} and III {[}P3{]} demonstrated four-class spatial metric
distortion, tidal splitting, and critical horizon collapse on a single
\(O_8\) shell (8 sites). However, directly simulating nested
\textbf{multi-shell lattices \(O_8^{\otimes K} \otimes M_8\)} with
Hamming shell depths \(d = 0, 1, \dots, K\) has been computationally
prohibitive: at \(K = 4\) (\(N = 32{,}768\)), a dense rank-3 tensor has
\(3.52 \times 10^{13}\) entries (\(\approx 280\text{ TB}\)).

In this Part V, we discover that under the triangle-loop product
\(\mathrm{Tri}\), each outer shell \(\{O_8, \delta_{000}\}\) closes
\textbf{exactly in 5 dimensions}, while the inner sector closes
\textbf{exactly in 4 dimensions} for \(\{M_8, M_{\mathrm{probe}}\}\) and
\textbf{6 dimensions} for the spin-2 source \(\{M_8, M_{\sigma_x}\}\).
Using SVD-based zero-truncation exact closure engines (no randomness),
we establish six machine-precision results:

\subsection{1. Introduction: The Computational Wall and 5D×4D/6D Exact
Closure}\label{introduction-the-computational-wall-and-5d4d6d-exact-closure}

Parts I--IV {[}P1--P4{]} treated a single \(O_8\) (1 center + 7 distant
sites) as the external space. Verifying radial gravity (\(1/r\)),
finite-speed causal propagation, and multi-shell time dilation
\(g_{00}(d)\) requires nested \(K\)-shell lattices
\(O_8^{\otimes K} \otimes M_8\) with Hamming distances
\(d = 0, 1, \dots, K\) (shell multiplicities \(\binom{K}{d} 7^d\)).

Dense storage scales as \(8^{3(K+1)}\): \(1.34\times 10^8\) entries
(\(\sim 1\text{ GB}\)) at \(K=2\), \(6.87\times 10^{10}\)
(\(550\text{ GB}\)) at \(K=3\), and \(3.52\times 10^{13}\)
(\(280\text{ TB}\)) at \(K=4\). Exploiting the tensor-product
homomorphism
\(\mathrm{Tri}(A\otimes B)=\mathrm{Tri}(A)\otimes\mathrm{Tri}(B)\)
{[}P1{]} and 7-axis imaginary isotropy, we find that: - Each outer shell
\(\{O_8, \delta_{000}\}\) closes \textbf{exactly in 5 dimensions} under
\(\mathrm{Tri}(X,Y,Z)\); - The inner sector closes \textbf{exactly in 4
dimensions} for \(\{M_8, M_{\mathrm{probe}}\}\) and \textbf{6
dimensions} for \(\{M_8, M_{\sigma_x}\}\).

This enables zero-truncation exact evolution up to \(K=4\;(N=32{,}768)\)
in seconds. Each claim below is tagged as \textbf{{[}Known{]}},
\textbf{{[}Part I--IV Established{]}}, \textbf{{[}Part V Verified{]}},
or \textbf{{[}Hypothesis / Unverified{]}}.
