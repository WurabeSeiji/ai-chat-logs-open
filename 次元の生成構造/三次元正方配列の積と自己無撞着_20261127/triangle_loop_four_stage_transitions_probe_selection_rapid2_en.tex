\subsection{Abstract}\label{abstract}

In our first rapid communication, we introduced the
\textbf{triangle-loop product}
\(\mathrm{Tri}(T)_{ijk} = \sum_{p,q,r} T_{ipq} T_{qjr} T_{rpk}\) on
cubic arrays without background spacetime, external operators, division,
or randomness, showing that only quaternions (\(N=4\)) and octonions
(\(N=8\)) survive as period-2 crystals and that a single-cell defect in
the coupled large crystal \(N=64\) (\(O_8 \otimes O_8\), 262,144
entries) yields a 512-cell composite remnant with bimodal view-angle
splitting.

extend exact full-\(64^3\) tracking up to update step \(\tau = 10,000\).

supercooled time-crystal intermediate phase}, followed by

\texttt{10.5281/\allowbreak{}zenodo.24614040}), we study the background-free
self-updating map on a cubic array
\(U \in \mathbb{R}^{N\times N\times N}\) (or

denote the update step strictly by \textbf{\(\tau\)} throughout this
report.

(\(O_8 \otimes O_8\)) stabilized into a 512-cell composite remnant over
\(\tau = 70\text{--}500\). Here we answer three open questions: (1) What
is the exact algebraic structure and self-repair mechanism of this
512-cell state? (2) How does it interact with nearby probes of different
spin, chirality, and localization? (3) Does the 512-cell state undergo
further phase transitions at larger \(\tau\)?
