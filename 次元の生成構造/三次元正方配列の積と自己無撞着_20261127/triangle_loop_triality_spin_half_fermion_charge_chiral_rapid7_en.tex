\subsection{Abstract}\label{abstract}

We analyze the internal algebraic and dynamical fermionic structures of
the outer octonion crystal \(O_8\) and Layer-1 time crystal
\(H = O_8 \otimes M_8\) selected by the background-free real 3-tensor
triangle-loop product
\(\mathrm{Tri}(T)_{ijk} = \sum_{p,q,r} T_{ipq} T_{qjr} T_{rpk}\) at
\(N = 64\) (\texttt{tri\_loop\_n64\_triality\_charge\_v0.py}, zero
randomness). Three exact results are established to machine precision:

\begin{enumerate}

\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{28D \(Spin(8)\) Triality and Spin-\(1/2\) Sign Flip (\(-I\)
  under \(2\pi\) Rotation)}: The 3-index triality invariance system
  (\(512\times 84\)) on \(O_8[i, j, k]\) has \texttt{rank\ =\ 56} and an
  exact invariant kernel of dimension \textbf{\texttt{28}} (max zero
  singular value \texttt{4.489e-15}, finite rotation error
  \texttt{8.934e-16}). Under a \(2\pi\) rotation in the spatial
  \((e_1, e_2)\) plane, the vector representation returns to \(+I\)
  (\texttt{7.622e-16}), whereas both left- and right-handed spinors flip
  sign strictly to \textbf{\(R_s(2\pi) = -I\) (\texttt{1.584e-14}) and
  \(R_c(2\pi) = -I\) (\texttt{1.793e-14})}, returning to \(+I\) only
  after \(4\pi\).
\item
  \textbf{CAR Fermion Algebra (Pauli Exclusion) and One-Generation
  Charge Spectrum (\(1:3:3:1\))}: From the left-multiplication Clifford
  algebra \(\{L_a, L_b\} = -2\delta_{ab}I\) (error
  \textbf{\texttt{0.000e+00}}) and the three color complex planes
  \((e_1,e_6), (e_2,e_5), (e_3,e_4)\) induced by fixing \(e_7\), three
  fermionic ladder operators \(\alpha_1, \alpha_2, \alpha_3\) satisfy
  exact canonical anticommutation relations
  \(\{\alpha_m, \alpha_n\} = 0\) (Pauli exclusion \(\alpha_m^2 = 0\))
  and \(\{\alpha_m, \alpha_n^\dagger\} = \delta_{mn}I\) (both error
  \textbf{\texttt{0.000e+00}}). The charge operator
  \(Q = \frac{1}{3}\sum_{m=1}^3 \alpha_m^\dagger \alpha_m\) commutes
  strictly with all 8 \(SU(3)\) gluon generators
  (\(\|[\Lambda_A, Q]\| = \mathbf{0.000\times 10^0}\)) and decomposes
  the 8 states into \(Q = 0\) (singlet, \(C_2=0\): \(\nu\)-type),

  \(C_2=0\): \(e^+\)-type)---an exact
  \textbf{\texttt{1\ :\ 3\ :\ 3\ :\ 1}} binomial degeneracy matching the
  \(\mathbb{Z}_2^3\) spatial harmonic law of Rapid Reports 3 and 6.
\item
  \textbf{Dynamical Chiral Splitting Driven by Inner \(M_8\)}: On

  (\(O_8 \leftrightarrow O_8^T\)) leaves \(\|P\|\) invariant, whereas
  reversing the inner time-crystal rotation
  (\(M_8 \leftrightarrow M_8^T\)) splits \(\|P\|\) from
  \texttt{0.046873} to \texttt{0.046212} (\(-1.41\%\)).
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{1. Introduction: From Bosons and Spacetime to Fermions and
Electric
Charge}\label{introduction-from-bosons-and-spacetime-to-fermions-and-electric-charge}

Rapid Communications 1--6 {[}1{]} established Hurwitz crystal selection,
four-stage entanglement cascades, \(SO(3)\) spin selection,
\(G_2 \supset SU(3)\) gauge dimensions, four emergent quantum theorems,
4-class spherical metric distortion with critical collapse, and Hurwitz

algebraic origin of fractional/integer electric charges

\(\mathbb{Z}_2^3\) spatial harmonics, and (iii) dynamical left/right
chiral asymmetry. Here we resolve all three at machine precision.
