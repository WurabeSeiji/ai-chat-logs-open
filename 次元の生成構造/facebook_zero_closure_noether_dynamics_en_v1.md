What I wanted to study this time was dynamics. Up to now I have taken two basic conditions — zero closure ΣXn²=0, the vanishing of a sum of complex squares, and finite recurrence U^N=I, returning to the start after N applications — and asked how much physical structure emerges from the relations among waves alone, with no background spacetime and no ready-made symmetry placed at the start. The previous paper produced most of the symmetries. But a big problem remained: how do things move in that world?

The trigger was Noether's theorem. Where there is a symmetry there is a conservation law: shift time and the laws stay the same, energy is conserved; shift space, momentum; rotate, angular momentum. The idea sits deep in the dynamics of modern physics. My model, however, places neither time nor space at the start. All there is is the closed relation ΣXn²=0. So where would Noether's theorem come from?

The key was equal-amplitude waves. If the amplitudes are equal, the only essential quantity distinguishing the waves is the phase. So I considered advancing only the phases a little without breaking zero closure. From the phase differences of neighbouring waves an oriented discrete current J=A²sin(φj−φi) and a discrete action emerge, and the stationarity condition of the action is precisely a discrete continuity equation ΣJ=0. This is a discrete Noether conservation law that holds exactly at finite resolution. No continuous derivative, no time derivative, no partial differential equation has been used so far.

The central claim of the paper is this. In ordinary field theory one first posits a rule that rewrites a state into the next state and calls it time evolution. In my axiom system that is not allowed. Self-consistency is a foundational condition, so if the updated state leaves zero closure or any other admissibility condition, that update rule is simply not a dynamics of this theory. The question is not whether one can produce a next state, but whether one can produce it without breaking the axioms. By projecting the force onto the tangent space of zero closure and, at finite steps, retracting back onto the manifold, a self-map was constructed that preserves ΣXi²=0 exactly at every iteration. Dynamics can be realised as a map that keeps the axioms, rather than by breaking an axiom and re-imposing it afterwards — that is the conclusion.

One point is easily misunderstood. The parameter s of this self-map is not physical time. It is a construction-and-selection parameter for reaching a self-consistent configuration; physical time t appears, as derived in the previous paper, as the Lorentz readout of an unobservable complex axis it. The fixed point of the self-map is a condition on the whole configuration including the time axis, and read in the Lorentz readout it is the field equation □φ=0. The discrete self-map and the continuum field equation coexist without privileging time at the foundation.

Then, as the resolution is made finer, N→∞, the discrete current becomes a continuous phase gradient, the discrete continuity equation becomes ∂μJμ=0, and the discrete action becomes the standard field action. The order is reversed. Normally: continuous spacetime, then differential equations, then conservation laws. In this model: discrete closure, then finite phase differences, then higher resolution, then continuous conservation structure. Continuous physical laws may be the limit of a discrete structure seen at very fine resolution.

What was unexpected came next. If each vertex may re-choose its phase origin independently, comparing vertices requires a compensating quantity on each edge; it satisfies the gauge transformation law of a U(1) connection, and the loop sum on a simplex face is a gauge-invariant curvature. Vertex: phase field; edge: connection; face: curvature. In the continuum limit the covariant derivative and a Maxwell-type action appear, and with multi-component vertex states the loop product yields the non-abelian Yang–Mills term. Connecting further to the five complex degrees of freedom and the 3⊕2 decomposition of the previous paper, the trace-zero condition fixes the hypercharge ratio, and the dual of the one-body side together with the oriented two-body relations, 5+10=15 components, decomposes directly into the 15 left-handed Weyl components of one Standard-Model generation (dc, L, uc, Q, ec). Cancellation of all anomalies was verified directly. SU(5) is not assumed: the known 5̄⊕10 content was reached in the order six-axis zero closure, five degrees of freedom, 3⊕2. The remaining chirality selection was identified with the two-channel selection system of earlier papers and reduced to a minimal form in which one side is spontaneously selected from left-right symmetric equations.

This time I deliberately approached the region where present standard physics succeeds with extremely high precision — the continuum limit N→∞ and the regime of large curvature radius, locally almost flat. I am not saying the standard theory is wrong. Rather the opposite: the possibility that has come into view is that one may explain why the standard theory is that accurate from the discrete structure one level below it. What I will examine next is whether this road really continues unbroken to Maxwell's equations and on to relativity.
