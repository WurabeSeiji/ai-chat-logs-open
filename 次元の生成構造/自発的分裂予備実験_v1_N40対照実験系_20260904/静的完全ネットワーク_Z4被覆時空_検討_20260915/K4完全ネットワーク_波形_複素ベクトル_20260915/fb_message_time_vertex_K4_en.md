A Finite Complete-Relation Closed System with Time Evolution as Vertices — Complete Analysis of the Minimal Exact System N=1, K=4

Details in the comment

Instead of advancing time from outside, all distinct phase states are placed as ordinary vertices.
Turning the four phase states into vertices yields the complete graph K4 with 4 vertices and 6 relations.
This minimal exactly closed system is analyzed without dropping a single state or relation.
For the even harmonic m=2 the harmonic period degenerates to 2; for the odd m=3 it stays 4.
The squared sum is exactly Q_loop=4 for even m and Q_loop=0 for odd m.
This parity difference is a corollary of the divisibility theorem Q(m;K)=K·1[K|2]+K·1[K|2m].
The same K4 splits in distance geometry and squared closure purely by its internal harmonics.
Mathematical results are explicitly separated from physical hypotheses (photon, time).
The next task: can temporal order be reconstructed from the complete relations alone, without order labels?
