Deriving the Coulomb-type inverse-square force from a product readout and an area clock

Details in the comments

A Coulomb-type inverse-square force can be written with one state and one relation alone
The interaction may stay linear; the inverse square comes from the readout and the clock, not from the law
If position too is a product readout, exactly three readouts remain, and the Pythagorean theorem appears as an identity
Fixing the clock so that the swept area is constant makes time the accumulation of distance, not an independent degree of freedom
The centre of force moves to the focus, and the orbit equation is the conserved quantity of the first thought experiment itself: Kepler's first law
Attraction and repulsion appear within one and the same linear law as a difference of sectors, with no charge sign given from outside
Neutrality has a place from the start: when the two null directions are orthogonal, the coupling vanishes for every orbit
There is a negative result too: coupling and angular momentum are proportional, so a collision orbit with nonvanishing coupling cannot be represented
Two values cannot write a map, a mass ratio or a charge ratio; direction acquires meaning only with three bodies
Verification used 16 preregistered conditions plus 2 added afterwards, 110 symbolic checks all zero, and identical verdicts in a second environment
