Neither time nor space exists. All there is are the pairwise relations among N entities — M = N(N−1)/2 complex numbers that encode the "relations between waves" (a complete graph).

From this closed world of relations alone, with nothing added from outside, an inflation-like geometric expansion of order 10³⁰ happens all by itself. I have been reporting this. Now, at last, I have written down the dynamics that drive the expansion as an explicit equation — and, on top of that, found the mechanism by which four-dimensional singular directions (directions that look like spacetime) are born from it.

■ The dynamics of inflation is, in fact, this simple

The system is advanced by a rotation determined by phases only.

- Map:　z →　exp(Δτ K) z 　(K_ef ＝ A_ef sin(φ_f − φ_e),　Δτ ＝ 2π/N)

Decomposed for a single wave, the equation of motion is these two lines:

- Amplitude:　dr_e/dτ ＝ ½ Σ_f r_f sin2(φ_f − φ_e)
- Phase:　　 r_e dφ_e/dτ ＝ Σ_f r_f sin²(φ_f − φ_e)

What drives the amplitude is the second harmonic, sin2 of the phase difference. It vanishes only when the phase difference is a multiple of 90 degrees. So the starting point the system settles onto (the "floor") is fixed to a 90-degree configuration in which every wave lies on two orthogonal axes. And inflation is simply the exponential amplification of the one unstable direction of that floor:

- H⊥/H(t) ≈ (seed)² × |μ_max|^{2t}

where μ_max is the instability of the floor (the largest eigenvalue of its Jacobian). The expansion was not put in from outside; it was already written into the tilting floor.

■ But ignition requires a "cross"

The initial state that ignites inflation demands an extremely high, strict symmetry — a wave configuration shaped like a cross (every wave lined up on two orthogonal axes at 90 degrees).

■ The cross topples — this is what inflation really is

This cross undergoes spontaneous symmetry breaking. From a spread that is exactly upright and vanishingly close to zero (it keeps approaching zero even when the precision is pushed to 100 digits), it topples abruptly — like a thin rod stood on a mountaintop falling over — and acquires a large radius. The potential is a Mexican-hat: the apex (the symmetric cross) is an unstable maximum, and the valley is the stable point.

〔Insert image: the Mexican-hat figure　fig_mexican_hat_ssb_N40.png〕

The result is a cloud of waves spread out over a large circle, arranged radially — while keeping the four directions of the cross.

■ The best part to watch in the video

Turning this into 3D is the first video. At first glance it may not look like inflation. But the vertical axis is a logarithmic axis of "the size of the spread, H⊥/H," and you can see it racing up 30 orders of magnitude, from 10⁻³¹ to 0.1. A thin column rises from the bottom and opens into a single ring at the top — this "racing up and closing into a ring" is exactly inflation and symmetry breaking.

■ And four directions were born

As a result, four singular directions emerge. Whether these are x, y, z and time t, or the directions of a spatial radius of curvature R and a charge-like quantity Q, is a question for future work. But I have indeed found the mechanism by which, in a world with neither time nor space, inflation gives birth to four directional axes.
