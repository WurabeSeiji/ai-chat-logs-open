Design the search first, then run the first thought experiment

Details in the comments

I redesigned my search for the foundations of physics, starting from "how to search" rather than the answer
The design document separates principles, axiom candidates, design assumptions, and verification conditions, and counts the discovery of infeasibility as success
The first thought experiment under that design is now published
The starting point is only two nameless numbers (a,b) and one principle: a state alone cannot be read
Assuming one conserved readout, the way of reading is uniquely fixed to an area
The law becomes a single real-number rule, and the first readable dynamical quantity is acceleration - the operational content of the equivalence principle
The sign of the sum of squares is decided automatically, and elliptic orbits (rotating systems) and hyperbolic orbits (uniformly accelerated systems) appeared
The orbit constant C is exactly the radius of curvature
Verification declared its failure conditions in advance; the one item that actually failed is kept in the paper without alteration
