Building Spacetime and Elementary Particles Without Background Spacetime: A Thought Experiment with Bounded Hilbert Space and Octonions

Details in the comments

I have published a thought-experiment paper reconstructing spacetime and elementary particles solely from state relations in a bounded Hilbert space and algebraic structures, without assuming background spacetime
In the 4D Minkowski metric, photons satisfy ds = 0, corresponding to being stationary within 4D spacetime from the viewpoint of proper time τ
Central projection via imaginary time R² = t² + (iτ)² yields the space x² + y² + z² = R², connecting to de Sitter spacetime, the event horizon R, and gravity as local contraction of R by matter
Because complex numbers (2 degrees of freedom) cannot retain 3D spatial directions without external coordinates, we extend to quaternions (4 degrees of freedom) via the Cayley–Dickson construction
Quaternions formed from two conjugate complex numbers retain 3-direction phase gradients through the Hopf fibration S¹ ↪ S³ → S², which is mathematically isomorphic to the Bloch sphere of a single qubit
Extending further to octonions (8 degrees of freedom) via the Hopf fibration S³ ↪ S⁷ → S⁴ separates closed 4D spacetime S⁴ and 3 internal degrees of freedom
By Hurwitz's theorem, real normed division algebras exist only in dimensions 1, 2, 4, and 8, so this algebraic extension closes at octonions
Even starting from N² all-to-all couplings, we hypothesize that octonionic non-associativity, triality, and minimal ideals factorize the system into self-organized local condensates
Because 8 degrees of freedom alone may not suffice for mass hierarchies and particle species, integrating this framework with localized waves and harmonic circulation is explicitly left as an open problem
The paper provides a research-program overview that strictly separates established identities and theorems from working hypotheses and open problems
