The previous article ended with an honest report.

With every fluctuation assumption removed, an inflation-like rapid expansion still started on its own, and the directions stopped at three. But no new direction that could carry the electromagnetic force ever appeared, no matter how long we waited. Were the degrees of freedom missing? We recorded that as an open problem.

This time, the problem got its answer.

What was missing was not degrees of freedom — it was where we were looking. The electric force was not waiting to be born as a new direction. It was hiding, from the very beginning, inside the collisions between waves.

When two waves collide, the amount transferred in one collision is exactly the sum of two terms. No third term exists. This is not an experimental result; it is a mathematical identity.

The first term reads only the waves' magnitude. Measured at machine precision, its properties are: it composes by addition, has no sign, cannot be screened, and is not quantized. Item by item, this matches the inventory of gravity — mass adds, there is no negative mass, and it cannot be shielded.

The second term reads the waves' overlap: whether they share the same harmonics, and whether the shared phases are aligned. Its properties: it composes by multiplication, reverses direction when the relative phase is shifted by half a turn (plus and minus), can be screened, and is quantized onto discrete values. That is the inventory of the electric force, item for item.

The crucial point: we taught this system neither gravity nor electricity. No particle species, no charge labels, no coupling constants were put in from outside — only wave shapes and a collision rule. Yet the two grammars separate on their own, and the assignment cannot be read in reverse (reversed, you would get a gravity that flips when a phase shifts, and an electricity that cannot build plus and minus — contradictions).

There was even a value attached. Writing R for the reflection rate of the exchange, there is a special exact point where the transmitted fraction 1−R = sin²(23π/124) = 0.302822…, matching the dimensionless elementary charge of physics (about 0.302822) to 16 parts in 100 million.

The trilogy also offers a structural candidate answer to why gravity is 10 to the 40 times weaker than electricity: the electric coupling is an angle, boxed into a narrow band by a ceiling and by quantization; the gravitational coupling is a ratio of magnitudes, which has no floor. A long-range force that is weaker by orders of magnitude can live only in the magnitude grammar.

We also state plainly what we cannot yet claim. This is the stage of grammars, before a background spacetime stands up — we do not claim it is real gravity and electromagnetism themselves. Why the elementary-charge angle chooses a division into 124 is still open; the mystery has been narrowed to a single question: why is the observation clock commensurable with 248?
