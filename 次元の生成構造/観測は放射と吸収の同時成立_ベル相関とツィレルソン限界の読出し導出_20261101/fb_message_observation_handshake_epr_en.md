Where did Einstein's "spooky action at a distance" go? Read observation as a handshake of emission and absorption, and the EPR paradox stops being a paradox

Details in the comments

The single word "observation" mixes three interactions: one that switches the partner's state, a weak readout that only shifts the phase, and decoherence that severs the phase relation. The substance of observation is the second
With no background spacetime, I started from distance. Distance is the ratio of a shared viewing angle to the partner's size, and only ratios are readable. One cannot observe oneself (viewing angle zero), and the overall scale is never readable
An interaction is a single relation in which emission and absorption hold at once (a handshake); the speed of light appears on the readout side as a conversion factor between local clocks
From these two and a harmonic order n alone, the form of the Born rule (probability = amplitude times its conjugate), the correlation E = −cos nθ, the CHSH bound 2√2 and no-signaling follow in a few lines
The bound is 2√2, neither 2 nor 4, because the readout is bilinear and normalized to unit length. The values agree with standard theory; what differs is what was assumed
For a localized wave the correlation is the autocorrelation of the pulse shape: a single tone is the quantum end, a rectangular pulse is a local hidden-variable model, the classical end. Localization and non-local correlation trade off
EPR is read not in spin but, as in the original paper, in position and momentum. The pair is a closure q_A + q_B = 0 at the source, and "positions sum to zero" and "momenta sum to zero" hold at once, as an identity, before any measurement
A single end has no value; reading A does nothing at B; the correlation depends neither on the order of the readouts nor on the distance. "Fixed instantaneously" has no frame-independent meaning
EPR's perfect correlation and locality are both correct; only the third step, attributing a relational quantity to one end, was wrong. Neither Einstein's "incomplete" nor the textbook "action at a distance" is needed
The existence of a destructive readout is placed as a known fact (resonant absorption, photodetection, homodyne detection); its mechanism is a separate task
