# Can Physics Be Read Out from a Single Multiplication, Without Placing Spacetime or Force Fields? — The Forms of Two Equations Were Inside the Product of Octonions

In yesterday's article, I wrote about rewriting the one-line formula of quantum chromodynamics (QCD), the theory of the strong force, in octonions, which are sets of eight numbers.

This is the continuation. Next I tried to make the rewritten formula move. Being able to write a formula and solving how particles move from it are different things. What determines the motion are two equations: the Yang–Mills equation and the Dirac equation. So I began to think about which calculations with octonions could express these two.

While thinking, I noticed that **the direction was reversed**.

It is not that the standard theory is copied into octonions. When only the multiplication of octonions is placed, the forms of the two equations come out of it. And this happens even though neither spacetime nor the fields that carry forces (gauge fields) have been placed beforehand.

I have written up this realization as a hypothesis in a paper and published it on Zenodo (Concept DOI: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24387753). The verification is still to come. In this article I introduce the content step by step.

## 1. Only one rule is placed

I am studying whether spacetime, particles, and interactions can be read out from the relations among states alone, without placing a background spacetime from the start.

This time, only one rule is placed.

　States are represented by octonions. The multiplication of octonions determines the next state.

No gauge field is placed. Neither the kinds of particles, nor the kinds of forces, nor a table of who acts on whom is given from outside. Only the initial state is given from outside.

## 2. Multiply three, and read the real part

An octonion consists of eight numbers: one real part, and seven parts that become −1 when squared.

The multiplication of octonions has an unusual property. When three are multiplied, the result changes depending on which two are multiplied first. Yet the real part of the result alone does not change, whatever the order. With four or more, the real part changes too.

In other words, the real part is determined independently of the order of multiplication only up to three. This is not something I chose; it is a property of the octonions.

So I multiply three octonions and read the real part.

　c = (the real part of o_A × o_B × o_C)

This single number is the main character this time.

## 3. The first reading: the form of Yang–Mills

In yesterday's article I touched on the method of calculating on a lattice (lattice QCD). There, the variables placed on the lines of the lattice are multiplied along a closed path. The amount by which one fails to return to the start after going around becomes the strength of the force field.

Multiplying three octonions has the same form. If the product of the three returns exactly to 1, it is closed. I call the amount by which it fails to return the closure remainder. The calculation gave the following.

　c ≈ 1 − (the square of the closure remainder) ÷ 2

This has the same form as the expression for the energy of the force field in Yang–Mills theory.

Furthermore, when the seven-part directions of the three octonions are aligned, no closure remainder arises. Only when the directions differ does the difference from exchanging the order of multiplication remain. The former is the type of light (electromagnetism), and the latter is the type of the strong force. The difference comes from whether the directions are aligned.

## 4. The second reading: the defect becomes, as it stands, the form of Dirac

Here there is a difficulty. In a theory that handles force fields, the result of carrying out transformations in succession must again be a single transformation. Octonions do not satisfy this. Seen as Yang–Mills, this is a defect.

Yet this same property was the required property in the other equation.

In the equation of electrons and quarks (the Dirac equation), there appear tools called gamma matrices, which have a special rule of multiplication. The operation of multiplying by an octonion from the left satisfies this rule all by itself. The property that, when carried out twice, it becomes the calculation expressing the spreading of a wave is also there from the start.

And the same triple multiplication can be read in the following two ways.

- Read all three as "links" → the form of Yang–Mills
- Read the middle one as a "link" and the two ends as "matter" → the form of Dirac

Two equations were not prepared separately and copied into octonions. They are **different readings of one multiplication**.

## 5. The third reading: the same number is a distance

There is one more thing I noticed. This number is measuring a distance.

　(the separation between o_A × o_B and the conjugate of o_C) squared = 2 − 2c

The conjugate is what you get by reversing the signs of the seven parts.

The product of two points to a certain position. The separation between that position and the third can be read from c. It is the same as triangulation, which determines the position of a third point from two points.

The distance is not given from outside. At the moment it was decided to read the real part of the multiplication, the distance was in.

## 6. Partners are not chosen. The distance becomes the strength of the bond

Here one problem remains: out of many states, which three are to form a set?

If someone chooses and specifies the sets, that is giving something from outside. On the other hand, if all sets are added with the same weight, the content peculiar to the octonions cancels out neatly and disappears.

My answer is this. Do not choose. Compute all combinations. And let the distance just read become, as it stands, the strength of the bond. With a distant partner, the result is simply that there is almost no bond.

In this way, the property that only near things act on one another comes out as a result, not as a promise placed at the start.

To summarize, the single number c plays three roles.

- Action (the form of Yang–Mills, the form of Dirac)
- Distance
- Strength of the bond

The standard theory first places spacetime, and on it computes how a force propagates according to distance. Here it is the reverse. From the multiplication of states, the strength of the bond is computed. What is read from it is distance, and is spacetime.

**Computing the strength of the force is, as it stands, reading out spacetime**. This is the center of the hypothesis this time.

The form of the action and the distance were checked by calculation. That it becomes the strength of the bond, and that spacetime can be read from it, are hypotheses. I will verify them from here on.

## 7. Size is made with harmonics

A single octonion has an angle corresponding to position, but no extent. To make a state gathered in one place, like a particle, waves are superposed.

What is used are harmonics. Taking as the base a wave that goes exactly once around a closed circumference, and superposing finer waves of twice, three times, and so on, gives a shape that rises in only one place. The center of the rise is at the same place as the center of the base wave. The more waves are superposed, the narrower the width.

I think that what determines the strength of the bond is not the distance itself but how large the partner appears (the viewing angle). If far but large, strong; if near but small, weak. Here the form of the formula is not yet settled into one.

## 8. The ruler, too, is a state

Finally, gravity.

In general relativity, gravity is the distortion of spacetime. I think that what is distorted is not the states themselves but the ruler that measures the states. When a globe is copied onto flat paper, the scale stretches toward the edges. Nothing on the globe has changed. What changed is the scale.

But if no background spacetime is placed, there is no place outside to put the ruler either. The ruler, too, must be one of the states.

Then a mechanism by which only the states playing the role of the ruler are distorted should be inside the same multiplication. As to what that ruler is, I arrived at two hypotheses.

- First: divide the eight numbers of the octonion into four and four. One half is the ruler
- Second: on the real part of the octonion rides a plain wave that has no direction. That is the ruler. This wave gives the whole universe a scale of equal intervals. It closely resembles the Higgs field

Which is right? Are both right, differing only in the way of reading? Are both wrong? I do not know yet. Deciding this is the next task.

## 9. What was checked, and what is to be checked

What was checked

- That the real part of the triple multiplication does not depend on the order of multiplication
- That this number takes the form of Yang–Mills
- That multiplying from the left satisfies the rule required by the Dirac equation
- That the same number is a distance

The formulas of the paper were checked numerically, in 57 items, with the attached program.

What is to be checked

- Whether the same forms come out just by not choosing partners and letting the distance be the strength of the bond
- Whether three-dimensional space and time can be read out from there
- Whether the differences in strength among the four forces come out of a single formula of the viewing angle
- The concrete form of the calculation that advances a state to the next state
- Which of the two hypotheses about the ruler is right

The parts used are all already known: the properties of octonions, the theory on a lattice, the rule of the gamma matrices. There is no new theorem. The content this time is that, when known parts are placed side by side, they can be read in this way. Precisely because the parts are known, there was no need to re-establish each one, and I could concentrate on the reading.

## 10. I wrote it in the order in which I thought

This paper is written not from the conclusion but in the order in which I thought. If only the conclusion is seen first, the story looks like a leap. Each step along the way is small, and the reader can check it for themselves.

The thought experiment was carried out as a dialogue with an AI (Claude). Along the way, misreadings occurred many times: giving something from outside, picturing a smoothly connected space first, reading the whole with an approximation that holds only in a small range. These too are written, as they are, at the places where they occurred, because readers will misread in the same way at the same places.

I would be grateful if you would point out errors or offer advice.
