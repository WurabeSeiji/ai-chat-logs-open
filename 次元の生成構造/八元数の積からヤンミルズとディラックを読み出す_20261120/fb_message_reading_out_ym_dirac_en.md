Reading Out Yang–Mills and Dirac from the Product of Octonions: A Thought Experiment in the Reverse Direction

Details in the comments

As a continuation of the preceding paper, which wrote the formula of QCD in octonions, I took up its dynamics, noticed that the direction was reversed, and published a paper setting this out as a hypothesis
Only one rule is placed: states are represented by octonions, and the product of octonions determines the next state. No background spacetime, no gauge field, and no particle species are given from outside
The real part of the product of three octonions is determined independently of the order of multiplication. This single number plays three roles: action, distance, and coupling coefficient
Read with all three as "links", it takes the form of the Yang–Mills action, the square of the closure remainder
Read with the middle one as a "link" and the two ends as "matter", it takes the form of the Dirac action. The defect that the octonions do not form a group is, as it stands, the property of the gamma matrices
The same number is also the distance, measured by triangulation, between the position pointed to by the product of two and the third. The distance is not given from outside
Partners are not chosen: all combinations are computed, and that distance is taken as the strength of the coupling. The center of the hypothesis is that computing the coefficient of the action is, as it stands, reading out spacetime
What bears the distortion of gravity is not the states but the ruler, and since there is no background, the ruler too is a state. Two hypotheses about that ruler are set up
The form of the action and the distance were checked with the attached program (57 items). The coupling coefficient, the readout of spacetime, and the hypotheses about the ruler are unverified; the verification is still to come
All the parts are known, and there is no new theorem. It is an interpretation paper: when the known parts are placed side by side, they can be read in this way
