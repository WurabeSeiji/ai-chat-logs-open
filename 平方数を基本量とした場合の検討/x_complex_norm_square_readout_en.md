The conjugate complex number is really just a sum of squares.

(x+iy)(x−iy)=x²+y²

i cancels, leaving Pythagoras. In many dimensions it lands on an equal-footing sum of squares too — a short observational note, just posted. With a small i²=−1 twist at the end.
